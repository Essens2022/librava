Load the pot in the morning, come home to a house that smells like dinner.

After a long day, the last thing you want is an hour at the stove. Your slow cooker can do the work for you, but only if the recipes are right: real flavor, tender meat, and times that match your day instead of someone else's.

The Easy Slow Cooker Cookbook gives you more than 80 dependable set-and-forget recipes with clear LOW and HIGH times, everyday supermarket ingredients and measurements for both US and UK kitchens. You will also learn the simple know-how that makes everything taste better: when browning is worth it, how to layer the pot, how to thicken sauces and how to keep food safe.
