Watercolor for Beginners Made Easy

Simple Techniques, Color Mixing and 15 Step-by-Step Paintings of Flowers, Landscapes and Everyday Things to Build Your Confidence

by Anna Lindgren

€4.97 · eBook (EPUB)

Watercolor is not about talent. It is about water, timing and a few simple techniques anyone can learn.

You buy a set of paints, wet a brush and the colors run everywhere. The sky turns muddy, the edges go hard, and the paper buckles. Most beginners give up right there, convinced they have no talent.

Watercolor for Beginners Made Easy explains what is really happening on the paper and how to control it. You will learn the materials that matter, how much water to use, how to mix clean colors and the core techniques step by step, then put it all together in 15 relaxing projects, from a soft sky wash to a loose floral greeting card.
