You planned your money for retirement. Now plan the part that makes you happy.

The farewell party is over, the alarm clock is off and the first weeks feel like a long holiday. Then the questions start. Who am I without my job? Where did my friends go? What do I actually do on a Tuesday? And how do we share the house all day without driving each other crazy?

The Happy Retirement Playbook is a warm, practical guide to designing the life you want after work. You will find a new rhythm, rediscover purpose, build friendships, stay strong and sharp, enjoy family without being taken for granted, travel smarter and feel calm about money, one chapter at a time.
