Fragrant, comforting curries at home that taste better than takeout.

You love curry night, but making it yourself feels complicated. Long spice lists, unfamiliar steps and fear of getting the heat wrong send you back to the takeout menu, even though you would love to cook it yourself.

The Easy Curry Cookbook is a warm, generous guide from a cook who learned in her grandmother's kitchen and a busy family takeout restaurant and now teaches beginner curry classes. With a simple five-stage method, supermarket ingredients and clear heat labels, you cook chicken, lamb, seafood and vegetable curries, comforting dal, noodle bowls, fluffy rice and homemade breads.
