30 Calm Bedtime Stories for Ages 3 to 7 About Kindness, Courage, Friendship and Feelings, to Help Little Ones Relax and Fall Asleep

Thirty soft, original stories that wind little ones down instead of winding them up.

Some bedtime books are too exciting, some are too long, and some are just a little bit scary. This one is written for the last few minutes of the day: gentle characters, small everyday problems, a soft line your child can say along with you, and an ending that always settles into sleep.

Sleepy Time Stories for Kids brings together thirty brand-new stories for ages 3 to 7, each about 5 to 7 minutes read aloud. A shy lighthouse cat, the smallest star in the sky, a fox who finds a lost mitten and a sleepy night train help children feel brave, kind and calm, without a single lecture.
