A great lawn is not about more products. It is about doing a few simple things at the right time.

Your grass is patchy, the weeds keep winning and every bag at the garden center promises a miracle. You water, you feed, you mow and still your lawn looks tired next to the neighbors.

Lawn Care Made Simple is a down-to-earth guide from a former golf course and sports field grounds crew member who later ran a small lawn service. You learn to identify your grass, read your soil, mow at the right height, water deeply, feed at the right time, handle weeds without panic, repair bare patches and follow a simple calendar for cool season and warm season lawns.
