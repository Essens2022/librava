How to Build Habits That Stick

Start With 5 Minutes a Day and Follow a 66-Day Plan, With 21 Fill-In Worksheets, 12 Ready-Made Starter Routines and a Restart Protocol

by Nora Whitlock

€2.99 · eBook (EPUB)  |  €2.99 · Digital book (PDF)

You start strong on January 2, and by the 12th it is already over? You do not lack willpower. You lack a plan built for ordinary days: the ones with rain, a headache, kids and deadlines.

This ebook teaches you to build one habit at a time , in a version so small you cannot say no. Every action comes in three sizes: a minimum for your worst days, a five-minute base and a bonus for when you feel like it. A skipped day does not send you back to zero: you get a short protocol to restart without arguing with yourself.

What you will find inside

What research says about how long it really takes (66 days on average, from 18 to 254) and why the 21-day myth fails

Five filters to choose the right habit, and the "when... then" sentence with a plan B and plan C

How to lower friction at home

A printable 66-square tracker and a check threshold of 10 out of 14

Off days, relapses, bad habits to replace, partners and family

Mornings, evenings, money, movement and study, plus twelve habits ready to copy

A 66-day plan in four phases and 21 worksheets to fill in by hand

Who it is for. Anyone who has tried to change something and quit, and wants a realistic method.

An honest note. General information, not medical or professional advice, especially for sleep, exercise, eating, addiction and heavy stress. The people in the examples are invented. Results vary and are not guaranteed.
