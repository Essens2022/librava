You do not need to be a "tech person" to build a website. You need clear steps, real code and someone patient beside you.

Maybe you want a page for your club, your small shop, your family history or your own portfolio. Maybe you have tried drag-and-drop builders and felt boxed in, or opened a tutorial and drowned in jargon by the second paragraph. The images will not show up, the layout collapses into a heap, one missing bracket breaks everything, and you start to wonder if coding is just not for you.

HTML and CSS for Beginners Made Simple is a cheerful, jargon-free guide from a former print shop worker who taught himself to build websites from free tutorials, built sites for local clubs and small shops, and now runs evening beginner web classes at a community learning center. He has made every mistake in this book at least twice, and he walks you from an empty folder to a finished, responsive, accessible website online, with every code example written to work exactly as typed and a "what you should see" after each one.
