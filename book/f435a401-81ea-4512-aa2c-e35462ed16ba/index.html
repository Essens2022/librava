Money Mindset: Change Your Relationship With Money

Where your money beliefs come from and how to rewrite them: 10 mental traps, 12 old sentences to replace, 4 conversations with the words ready and a 30-day plan

by Nathaniel Pryce

€2.99 · eBook (EPUB)  |  €2.99 · Digital book (PDF)

You earn enough, yet at the end of the month you wonder where it all went? It is rarely a math problem. It is a sentence you heard at nine years old, a mental account that doesn't hold up, a shame you never mention to anyone.

This ebook shows you how the mind works when cash is involved, with real studies explained without jargon and examples of invented but recognizable people. You will not find tips on what to buy. You will find a way to understand why you buy, avoid, postpone, compare and feel guilty.

What you will find inside

The four hidden scripts that steer your choices, and a worksheet to find your first memory of money

Ten mental traps, from scarcity to the pain of paying, from mental accounts to the ostrich effect, with calculations redone on each case

Four conversations to have with your partner, with the opening sentences already written

How to ask for a raise or raise your rate, with the words and the figures to prepare

Five questions to expose easy promises, and the official checks to make before you hand money to anyone

Twelve old sentences to rewrite, rules for big decisions and a 30-day plan, one small action a day

Who it is for. Anyone who wants to feel calmer about money without becoming a finance expert, and anyone who set themselves rules and saw them collapse after two weeks.

An honest note. This is general information and everyday psychology: it is not financial or psychological advice and does not replace a professional. The examples use invented people and illustrative figures. Results vary from person to person.
