You are already the one everyone asks to organize the party. It is time you were paid for it, properly.

You have run school fairs, office leaving dos and family milestones with a clipboard and a smile. People keep telling you that you should do this for a living. But the moment you imagine charging real clients, the questions pile up: what do I charge, what goes in a contract, what if the caterer cancels, what if a guest gets hurt, and who would ever hire me without a portfolio?

Start an Event Planning Business Made Simple is a warm, unflappable guide from a former hotel front desk supervisor and banqueting assistant who started charging for friends' parties and has spent many years planning weddings, milestone parties and small corporate days. She is funny about the disasters she has survived and strict about contracts, safety and never promising what a venue or vendor cannot deliver. Step by step you test the work, choose a niche, set up the business, protect yourself with clear contracts, price your services so they pay, win clients, manage vendors and budgets, build run sheets and run every event safely, from first enquiry to the final thank-you.
