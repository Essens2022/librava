A Practical and Compassionate Guide for Family Caregivers of a Loved One With Alzheimer's or Another Dementia, With Easy Ways to Communicate, Manage Daily Care and Behavior Changes, Keep Them Safe at Home, Plan Ahead and Look After Yourself Too, Including a Behavior Change Checklist, Home Safety Checklist, Missing Person Plan and 14 Questions to Ask the Doctor

You did not choose to become a dementia caregiver. But you can feel far less alone and far more in control.

Your mother asks the same question for the fifth time in an hour. Your husband insists you are not his wife. The stove was left on again, the paperwork is untouched, nights are broken and every conversation seems to end in an argument. You love them deeply, you are exhausted, and nobody has told you what to actually do next.

Dementia Caregiving Made Simple is a warm, honest and deeply practical companion from a former bank branch manager who cared for her mother through eight years of Alzheimer's disease while working full time and raising two teenagers, and who now co-leads a dementia carers' support group. You learn what dementia really is, how to talk so you are understood, how to handle daily care, sleep and behavior changes without blame, how to keep home safe, how to plan legally and financially while there is still time, how to build a care team and how to look after yourself through every stage.
