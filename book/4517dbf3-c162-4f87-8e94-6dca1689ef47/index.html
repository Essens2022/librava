The floor-kicking tantrum, the bedtime that takes two hours, the dinner that ends in tears. There is a calmer way through it.

You love your little one more than anything. But some days it feels like you are losing every battle, raising your voice more than you want and going to bed exhausted and guilty. The good news is that most toddler struggles are normal, and there are simple, kind ways to handle them.

Calm Parent, Happy Toddler explains what is going on inside your toddler's developing brain, helps you stay calm yourself and gives you clear steps for the big moments. Prevent many meltdowns before they start, handle tantrums and hitting without yelling, set limits that stick and turn bedtime, mealtimes and potty training into calmer parts of the day.
