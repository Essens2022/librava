Lawn Care Business Made Simple

How to Start a Lawn Mowing and Garden Maintenance Business, Choose Your Services and Equipment, Work Safely, Stay Legal and Insured, Price Every Job With Confidence and Build a Round of Loyal Customers, With a 30-Day Launch Plan and Ready-to-Use Checklists

You already love being outside with a mower. Here is how to turn it into a real, well-run local business.

Maybe you already cut a few lawns for neighbors and wonder if it could be more. But the questions stop you. What do you charge so you are not working for nothing? Which mower do you actually need? Do you need insurance, a license or a waste permit? Can you spray weeds? And how do you find customers close enough that you are not spending half your day driving?

Lawn Care Business Made Simple is a practical, cheerful guide from a former delivery driver who started mowing neighbors' lawns on Saturdays with an old push mower and a borrowed trailer and grew it into a small year-round lawn and garden maintenance business with one helper. It teaches the business: which services to offer, what equipment to buy, how to work safely, stay legal and insured, price every job with clear methods, find customers on tight routes, keep them for years and plan through every season.
