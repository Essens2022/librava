You are the person who spots the typo on the menu. That eye for detail can become real work from your own home.

But the questions pile up. What is the difference between proofreading and transcription? How do you pass the tests? What should you charge, and how do you know the offer in your inbox is not a scam?

Proofreading and Transcription From Home Made Simple is a calm, precise guide from a former legal secretary and typist who began transcribing from home when a back problem made commuting hard, added proofreading for students, authors and small businesses, and has worked from her spare room for twelve years.
