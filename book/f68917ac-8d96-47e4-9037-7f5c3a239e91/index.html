Your bonsai is a real tree. Treat it like one and it can outlive you.

Maybe someone gave you a little tree and the leaves are already dropping. Maybe you fear one wrong cut will ruin it, or you have read so many rules about soil and wire that you do not know where to start.

Bonsai for Beginners Made Simple is a patient, honest guide from a former tree surgeon who killed his first three bonsai and then spent twenty years learning from a local club. You learn which trees live indoors and which must live outside, how to water by reading the soil, how to prune, wire and repot safely and how to turn an inexpensive nursery shrub into your first bonsai.
