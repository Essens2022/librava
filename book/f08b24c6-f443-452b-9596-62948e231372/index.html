You do not have to be fast, thin or young to become a runner.

Maybe the last time you ran was at school, and it hurt. Maybe you tried an app, went out too fast and gave up in week two. Running looks like something other people do.

Running for Beginners Made Simple takes you from walking to running your first 5K (3.1 miles) in 10 weeks with gentle run/walk sessions, three times a week. You learn how to run easy, warm up, stay injury free, fit runs into real life and enjoy race day.
