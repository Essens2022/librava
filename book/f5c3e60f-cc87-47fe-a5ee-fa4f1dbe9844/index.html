66 Easy Savory Mediterranean Recipes and a Practical Eating Plan for Fatty Liver Disease (MASLD and NAFLD), With Less Added Sugar, Smart Swaps for Sugary Drinks, Label Reading Help, Everyday Activity Tips and Four Weeks of Liver-Friendly Meal Plans, With US and UK Measurements

Just been told you have fatty liver? You do not need a detox or a crash diet. You need simple, good food and habits you can actually keep.

The doctor said "fatty liver", handed you a leaflet and moved on. Then you went home and searched online, and found scary stories, miracle teas and cleanses that promise to flush your liver in seven days. You still do not know what to eat for breakfast tomorrow, or whether that soda in the fridge matters.

Fatty Liver Diet Made Simple is written by a former long-distance delivery driver who lived on drive-through meals and a bottle of cola in the cup holder, until a routine blood test and a scan showed fatty liver in his late forties. With his doctor keeping an eye on things, he learned to cook the Mediterranean-style food his wife grew up with, swapped sugary drinks for water and tea and started walking after dinner. He explains MASLD in plain words, busts the detox myths and gives you 66 easy savory recipes and four weeks of meal plans that support your liver alongside your doctor's care.
