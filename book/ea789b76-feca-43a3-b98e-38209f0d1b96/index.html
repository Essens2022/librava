Racing heart, shaky voice, mind going blank? You can learn to speak calmly and clearly, starting with your very next talk.

You know your stuff. But the moment you stand up in a meeting, present to your team or raise a glass at a wedding, the nerves take over. You rush, you read your slides, you forget the point you wanted to make, and you leave thinking you could have done so much better.

Speak Without Fear is a practical, kind guide to speaking with confidence. Learn why nerves happen and how to calm them, how to find your one clear message, how to structure any talk in minutes, and how to use your voice, body language and slides so people listen. Then handle questions, tough moments, meetings and video calls with simple, ready-to-use techniques and scripts.
