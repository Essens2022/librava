You are not broken, and you do not have to just live with it.

You laugh at a friend's joke and feel that small, warm, unwelcome trickle. You cross your legs before every sneeze, map every toilet in town, skip the dance class and the run you used to love and wonder whether this is simply what happens after babies, after menopause or after a certain age.

Pelvic Floor Health for Women Made Simple is a warm, judgment-free guide from a former primary school teacher and mother of three who started leaking when she laughed, sneezed or jogged after her third baby and kept quiet about it for years. When she finally saw her doctor and a pelvic health physical therapist, she was amazed how much simple daily exercises and better bladder habits helped, and she now gives relaxed talks for a local women's group and a new parents' group. You learn exactly where your pelvic floor is, how to squeeze and relax it properly, how to calm sudden urges, which habits quietly make leaks worse and when to get help, so you can feel more in control at every stage of life.
