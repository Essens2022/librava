Turn a rainy afternoon into a fizzing, bubbling, color-changing adventure with things you already have at home.

Kids are full of questions. Why do things float? How does a magnet work? Where do the bubbles come from? This book helps you explore the answers together, hands on, with simple experiments that are easy to set up and fun to talk about.

Amazing Science Experiments for Kids at Home is packed with 50 easy, safe experiments written by a former science teacher and parent who runs a weekend science club. Each one has a clear list of everyday materials, step-by-step instructions, a simple explanation of what is happening, safety notes for grown-ups and an idea to explore further.
