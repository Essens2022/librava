Stop cooking three dinners a night. Here is one safe, tasty meal everyone at your table can eat.

Your child has a food allergy, or two, or three, and every meal feels like a risk assessment. You read labels in the aisle until your eyes blur, you cook a separate plate for each person, and party invitations sit on the fridge because you do not know what to say to the host. You are exhausted, you are scared of getting it wrong, and you just want dinner to feel normal again.

The Easy Allergy-Friendly Family Cookbook is written by a school office administrator whose son had a severe reaction to peanut at two years old and whose daughter was later diagnosed with egg and milk allergies. After a year of three different dinners every night, she decided to cook one meal everyone could eat safely, and she now runs a support group for allergy parents at her local library. Calm, organized and honest about the fear, she shows you how to read labels in five countries, stop cross-contact at home, handle school, parties and restaurants, and cook 68 savory family recipes free from peanuts, tree nuts, milk, egg, wheat, soy, fish, shellfish and sesame, from crispy chicken nuggets and pizza to cottage pie, chili and lunchbox favorites.
