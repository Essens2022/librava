Just diagnosed with celiac disease? Dinner can still be normal, filling and safe.

You stand in front of the cupboard and everything looks like a threat. Soy sauce, stock cubes, sausages, gravy, even the toaster. Specialty bread costs a fortune and tastes like a sponge, every label feels like a puzzle and you are tired of eating a sad separate plate while everyone else enjoys real food.

The Easy Gluten-Free Cookbook is a calm, practical guide from a former school cook who was diagnosed with celiac disease in her thirties and then watched her teenage son get the same diagnosis. She learned the hard way about shared toasters, sneaky soy sauce and just a few crumbs. Now she cooks one gluten-free dinner for the whole family, and she shows you how to read labels with confidence, set up a safe kitchen and make 70 savory recipes built on rice, potatoes, corn, beans, eggs, meat and fish, with only a few labeled gluten-free products.
