Heard that eating differently might help you feel better, but drowning in miracle foods and scary lists? Start here, with real dinners.

Maybe your knees ache on the stairs, your doctor mentioned your heart, or you simply want to eat better as the years go by. You search "anti-inflammatory diet" and get superfood powders, banned-food lists, detox plans and recipes that need a specialist shop. Meanwhile it is six in the evening, everyone is hungry and you just want to know what to cook.

The Easy Anti-Inflammatory Cookbook is written by a former school cook who changed the way her family ate when her husband was told to look after his heart and her own knees began to ache. No hype, no supplements and no foods to fear: she explains in plain words what the research suggests, shows you the simple plate and a well stocked pantry, then gives you 66 savory recipes built on oily fish, beans and lentils, whole grains, colorful vegetables, olive oil, herbs and spices, from sheet pan salmon and sardine spaghetti to chickpea curry, tagine and Tuscan bean soup.
