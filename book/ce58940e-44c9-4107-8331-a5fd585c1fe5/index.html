67 Easy Nourishing Savory Recipes for a Healthy Pregnancy, With Foods to Avoid Clearly Explained, Nausea Friendly Meals, Iron and Calcium Rich Dinners, a Safe Fish Guide, Four Weeks of Meal Plans and Freezer Meals for After the Birth, With US and UK Measurements

Pregnant, hungry and confused about what is actually safe to eat? Here are clear answers and real dinners.

One website says the cheese is fine, another says never. Your aunt has opinions, your friend has different ones, and the leaflet is buried under the mail. Meanwhile you feel sick from four in the afternoon, the smell of onions sends you running and you are standing at the fridge wondering if anything in it is allowed. You want to look after your baby and yourself, but every meal feels like a test you might fail.

Pregnancy Cookbook Made Simple is written by a former school catering manager and mother of three who survived miserable morning sickness and a fridge door covered in a handwritten "can I eat this?" list, and who now runs relaxed cooking sessions for expectant parents. She explains what your body really needs, which supplements to ask about, which foods to avoid or handle with care and why, how to eat fish safely and how to get through nausea, heartburn and exhaustion. Then she gives you 67 savory recipes, from ginger chicken noodle soup and egg muffins to beef and bean chili, baked salmon, paneer curry and freezer-ready pies, so you can eat well and feel calm and confident at every meal, with your midwife or doctor always having the final word.
