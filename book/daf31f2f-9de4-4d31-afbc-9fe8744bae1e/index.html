Yellow leaves, droopy stems, another plant in the bin? It is not your fault, and it is easy to fix.

Most houseplants do not die from neglect. They die from too much water, the wrong light or a pot with no drainage. Once you understand the few simple things plants really need, keeping them happy becomes easy, even in a dim apartment or a busy life.

The Houseplant Rescue Guide explains light, water, soil, humidity and feeding in plain words, then helps you diagnose problems fast and choose plants that suit your home. You also get 40 easy plant profiles, from snake plants and pothos to monsteras and pet-safer picks.
