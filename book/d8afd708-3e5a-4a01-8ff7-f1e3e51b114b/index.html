You are more capable than your self-doubt lets you believe.

You stay quiet in meetings even when you have a good idea. You replay awkward moments for days, say yes when you mean no and compare yourself to everyone around you. Deep down you know you could do more, if only you felt sure of yourself.

Self-Confidence Made Simple is a kind, practical guide from a former shy librarian who now leads confidence workshops for adults. You learn what confidence really is, quiet your inner critic, build courage with small wins, speak up at work and in social settings, say no without guilt, handle criticism and mistakes and walk into big moments feeling steady.
