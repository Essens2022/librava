People keep saying you should sell your food. Here is how to do it without losing your savings or your licence.

You dream of a truck with your name on the side and a queue down the street. But the rules feel like a maze, used trucks hide expensive problems, and you have heard about vendors who bought a beautiful van only to find they could not legally park it, pass an inspection or make a penny on their best dish.

Food Truck and Market Stall Business Made Simple is an upbeat, practical guide from a former line cook who started with a folding gazebo and a two-burner grill at a weekend farmers market and has run a small food truck for many years. He is a little cheeky, obsessive about food safety and fanatical about knowing your numbers before you buy anything big. Step by step you test your idea small, pick a focused concept, choose and check a unit, get every permit, build food safety and allergen systems that pass inspection, cost your menu, find spots that pay, run a smooth service day, hire fairly and keep your money in order.
