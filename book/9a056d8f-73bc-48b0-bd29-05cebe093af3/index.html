Plan Your First Trip Alone, Stay Safe and Travel With Confidence at Any Age, With Step-by-Step Planning Guides, Budget Tips, Safety Checklists and Ways to Meet People Without Feeling Lonely

Stop waiting for someone to come with you. The world is ready when you are.

Your friends never have the same holidays, your partner does not share your wish list or you simply want to go. But the thought of traveling alone brings worries about safety, loneliness, eating alone and getting lost.

Solo Travel Made Simple is a candid, warm guide from a woman who took her first solo trip at 41, terrified, and has since traveled alone through more than 40 countries. You choose a beginner-friendly first destination, set a budget, plan step by step, find safe places to stay, pack light, stay safe without being scared and meet people so you never feel lonely.
