How to Turn a Spare Room, Annex, Whole Home or Holiday Property Into a Welcoming Vacation Rental Guests Love, With Clear Steps for Local Rules, Insurance and Taxes, Safety, Honest Listings, Photos, Pricing Methods, Cleaning Turnovers and Great Reviews, Plus 7 Guest Message Templates and a 30-Day Starter Plan

You have a spare room, an annex or a family cottage sitting empty. You could welcome travelers into it, calmly, safely and legally.

But the questions pile up fast. Do you need a permit? Will your insurance cover paying guests? What do you charge, what do you say when a guest cannot get in at midnight, and how do you clean between stays without losing your whole weekend? One wrong step could mean a fine, a refused claim or a review that stings for months.

Short-Term Rental Hosting Made Simple is a warm, practical guide from a former bed and breakfast housekeeper who rented out her own spare room, then a seaside cottage she inherited, and learned about permits, insurance and turnovers the hard way. Step by step you check every layer of rules, set up a comfortable and safe space, write an honest listing, take photos that show the real room, price with a clear method and run every stay with ready-made messages and checklists, so hosting feels calm instead of chaotic.
