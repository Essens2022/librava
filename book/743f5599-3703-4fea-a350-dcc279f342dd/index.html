Pick sun-warm tomatoes and fresh salad from your own balcony.

No garden? No problem. A sunny patio, balcony or doorstep can give you baskets of fresh food, if you know which pots, mix and plants to choose, and how to keep them happy through the season.

Container Vegetable Gardening Made Simple shows you how, step by step. You read your space, pick the right containers, mix and feed for healthy roots, start seeds or plants, water with confidence, then grow tomatoes, salads, peppers, beans, zucchini, potatoes, carrots, strawberries and herbs in pots, with gentle fixes for pests and a plan for the whole year.
