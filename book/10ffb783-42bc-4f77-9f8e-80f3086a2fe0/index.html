Anger Management Made Simple

Understand Your Triggers, Stay Calm in the Moment, Stop Snapping at the People You Love, Communicate Without Shouting and Repair Relationships After an Outburst, With a 30-Day Calm Practice Plan, Ready-to-Use Scripts and an Anger Log

You are not a bad person. You just have a short fuse, and you can change what happens when it burns.

You snap at your partner over nothing. You shout at the kids and then lie awake replaying their faces. You fire off the email, slam the door or go silent for days, and afterward the guilt is crushing. You promise it will be different next time. Then it happens again.

Anger Management Made Simple is a plain-spoken, honest guide from a former restaurant kitchen manager who shouted his way through his career until his young son flinched at his raised voice. He took a community anger course and now co-leads a peer support group. You learn to spot your triggers, read your body's warning signs early, calm down in the moment, swap hot thoughts for cooler ones, speak without shouting and repair relationships after an outburst, at home, with your kids, at work and on the road.
