Keep bees with confidence, not guesswork.

Beekeeping looks peaceful, until you open a hive for the first time and thousands of bees look back at you. What are you looking for? When should you add a box? What do you do when they start making queen cells?

Beekeeping for Beginners Made Simple walks you through your first hive step by step: how a colony works, choosing equipment and a site, getting bees, calm inspections, swarm control, varroa and disease, preparing for winter and harvesting your own honey.
