Golf for Beginners Made Simple

Learn the Grip, Swing, Short Game and Rules Step by Step, Avoid Common Mistakes and Break 100 With Confidence

Golf is not as hard as it looks once someone explains it in the right order.

You watch a friend hit a beautiful drive and think, how hard can it be? Then you try. The ball goes left, right, along the ground or nowhere at all. Online videos give you a hundred conflicting tips, and the rules seem written for lawyers.

Golf for Beginners Made Simple takes you step by step from your very first grip to playing a full round with confidence. You will build a simple, repeatable swing, learn the short game shots that save the most strokes, understand the rules and etiquette, and follow a 12-week plan designed to help you break 100.
