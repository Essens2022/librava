Crochet for Beginners Made Easy

Learn the Basic Stitches Step by Step, Read Patterns With Confidence and Make 12 Simple, Beautiful Projects, From Dishcloths to a Cozy Blanket

One hook, one ball of yarn and a quiet evening. That is all it takes to start.

Maybe you watched a few videos and ended up with a tangled knot. Maybe patterns look like a secret code full of brackets and asterisks. Or maybe you just want a calm, creative hobby that ends with something warm and beautiful in your hands.

Crochet for Beginners Made Easy teaches you from the very first slip knot. Every basic stitch is explained slowly, step by step, then you learn to read patterns, shape your work, change colors and fix mistakes. Twelve easy projects build your skills one at a time, from a simple dishcloth to a cozy granny square blanket.
