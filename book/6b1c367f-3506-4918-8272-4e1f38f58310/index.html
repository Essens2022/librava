You do not need to hit rock bottom to want to drink less. You just need a plan that works on a Thursday at 6 p.m.

You never decided to drink this much. One glass to unwind slowly became two, then most of a bottle. You wake at 3 a.m. with your heart racing, feel foggy at your Monday meeting and promise yourself tonight will be different, until the wine o'clock feeling arrives and the bottle is open before you have even put your keys down.

Drink Less Made Simple is a warm, practical and never preachy guide from a former events manager whose nightly glass of wine crept up over the years. She took a month off, then chose to drink much less and sometimes not at all, and now runs a friendly sober curious meetup and an alcohol-free drinks club at her local community center. With no labels and no judgment, you learn how much you really drink, choose a goal that suits you, set up your first week safely, spot your triggers, ride out cravings, enjoy great alcohol-free drinks and handle parties, partners and pushy friends, so you can feel more in control of your drinking on your own terms.
