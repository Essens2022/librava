How to Focus Better

A 14-Day Reset to Break the Phone Habit and Do Deep Work in 90-Minute Blocks, With 5 Worked Setups and 12 Copy-Ready Scripts

by Daniel Thornbury

€2.99 · Digital book (PDF)  |  €2.99 · eBook (EPUB)

You sat down to write one email. Forty minutes later it is still blank. Your phone shows 118 pickups before dinner, and you cannot say what you did with any of them. You are not lazy. Your attention is being pulled all day by things built to pull it, and willpower alone loses that fight.

This ebook skips the lecture and rebuilds the room around you, one small step a day, until paying attention is the easy choice. The Lamp Method has four rules: lock away what pulls you, aim at one task, make a block, park and protect.

Inside 21 chapters:

A day-by-day reset for 14 days, from 35 minutes on day 1 to a 60-minute block on day 14

A 20-question Leak Audit scored out of 40 that shows whether phone, place, people or body drains you most

Three notification tiers, app limits and the friction tricks that stop the thoughtless pickup

A block ladder that grows from 20 to 90 minutes, with the six parts of a good block

12 copy-ready scripts for your manager, colleagues, partner, child and doctor

Five worked setups: office, student, remote developer, parent and writer

Study methods, sleep and caffeine basics, relapse plans and a 30-day maintenance plan

Who it is for. Office workers, remote workers, students, freelancers, parents working at home and anyone who wants their evenings back.

An honest note. The people in the examples are invented and the results are illustrations, not promises. This ebook is general information, not medical advice, and chapter 17 explains when to see a professional.
