Pollinator Garden Made Simple

Create a Beautiful Garden Buzzing With Bees, Butterflies and Other Helpful Wildlife, With Native Plant Choices by Region, Season-by-Season Bloom Plans and Easy Projects for Any Yard, Balcony or Patio

Free · eBook (EPUB)  |  Free · Digital book (PDF)

Every flower you plant is a meal for someone small and important.

You have heard that bees and butterflies are in trouble and you want to help, but you are not sure which plants to choose, whether your small yard or balcony counts or how to start without a big budget.

Pollinator Garden Made Simple is a warm, practical guide from a former biology teacher who turned a bare suburban lawn into a wildlife garden. You meet the pollinators, read your garden, choose native plants for your region, plan blooms from early spring to late fall, design a patch that looks good, give pollinators places to live and garden without poisons on any scale.
