Turn Your Baking Into a Legal Small Business From Your Own Kitchen, Understand Cottage Food Rules, Register and Pass Inspections, Label Allergens Correctly, Price Your Bakes With Confidence and Sell at Markets, Online and Through Custom Orders, With Worksheets, Templates and a 90-Day Plan

You bake beautifully. Now find out how to sell it legally, safely and for a price that actually pays you.

Friends beg for your cakes and neighbors ask you to bake for every party. But you have no idea whether you are even allowed to take their money, labels and allergens feel terrifying, and every time you work out what you charge, it seems you are barely covering the butter, let alone your weekends.

Start a Home Bakery Made Simple is a warm, organized guide from a former school office manager who baked birthday cakes for every family on her street and turned it into a registered home bakery selling celebration cakes, cookies and bread from home and at a weekly market. She is honest about the boring paperwork and the 2 a.m. buttercream disasters and firm about allergens and hygiene. Step by step you check what your local rules allow, register and pass inspection, set up a safe kitchen, label allergens correctly, build a focused menu, price every bake properly and sell at markets, online and through custom orders, without burning out.
