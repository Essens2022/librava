"Everyone in my class has one." Now what?

Sooner or later every parent hears it. You want your child to fit in, stay reachable and learn to use technology well. You also worry about sleep, schoolwork, group chat drama, endless scrolling and strangers online. Most advice either panics or shrugs.

The First Phone Playbook gives you a calm, practical middle path. It helps you decide when your child is really ready, shows the options in between a smartphone and nothing, and walks you step by step through the conversations, the setup and the family rules, so the first phone brings more freedom and less conflict.
