You do not need longer walks. You need smarter ones.

If your daily walk feels pleasant but nothing changes, you are not doing it wrong. Your body simply got used to it. The fix comes from Japan: alternate 3 minutes of brisk walking with 3 minutes of easy walking, five times over. About half an hour, no gym, no running.

Walk Smarter, Not Longer turns the method behind the viral "Japanese walking" trend into a clear, safe plan for adults over 40. It explains what the original research from Shinshu University really did, shows you exactly how fast your "fast" should be, and walks you week by week through a 12-week program you can start today.
