Setting Boundaries Made Simple

How to Stop People Pleasing, Say No Without Guilt and Protect Your Time and Energy, With Kind and Clear Scripts for Family, Friends, Partners, Work and Difficult People, Calm Answers to Pushback and a 30-Day Boundary Practice Plan

by Laurel Kinsella

€2.99 · eBook (EPUB)  |  €2.99 · Digital book (PDF)

You are not too nice. You have just never been shown how to say no and still be kind.

You say yes before you have even checked your calendar. You cover the shift, host the dinner, lend the money and answer the late-night message, then lie awake replaying every request, tired, quietly resentful and wondering when it will finally be your turn.

Setting Boundaries Made Simple is a warm, honest guide from a former school office manager and the eldest of five who said yes to everyone for decades, burned out in her forties and now runs friendly evening workshops on saying no at her local library. No harsh "cut everyone off" advice: you learn where your people pleasing comes from, find out what you really need, say no calmly with ready-made words, sit through guilt and pushback and set loving limits with family, partners, friends, coworkers and difficult people, so you protect your time and energy and keep the relationships you value.
