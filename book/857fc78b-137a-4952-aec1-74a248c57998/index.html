Smartphones for Seniors Made Easy

A Friendly, Large-Print-Style Guide to Calls, Messages, Photos, Video Chats, Apps and Staying Safe, for Any Modern Phone

by Margaret Collins

€6.97 · Digital book (PDF)

Your new phone can bring your family closer every day. This book shows you how, one gentle step at a time.

The family gave you a smartphone. It is shiny and clever, and also a little confusing. The text is small, the buttons hide, and every time you touch the screen something unexpected happens. You would love to see the grandchildren on video and send photos, if only someone would explain it slowly.

Smartphones for Seniors Made Easy is that patient explanation. Written for any modern phone, it starts with the very basics and builds your confidence step by step. You will make the screen easier to see and hear, call and message, video chat with family, take and share photos, use helpful apps and stay safe from scams.
