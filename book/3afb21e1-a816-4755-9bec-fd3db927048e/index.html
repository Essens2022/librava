Fresh, healthy fish dinners you can cook with total confidence.

You know you should eat more fish, but standing at the counter feels confusing. What is fresh, how long will it keep and how do you cook it without drying it out or filling the house with smells?

The Easy Fish and Seafood Cookbook is a friendly, reassuring guide from a former fishmonger turned cookery teacher. You learn to buy, store and prep seafood, cook it perfectly with six simple methods and enjoy 70 savory recipes, from fifteen-minute dinners and salmon to mussels, chowders, fish tacos and seafood pasta, each with a swap line for other fish that work.
