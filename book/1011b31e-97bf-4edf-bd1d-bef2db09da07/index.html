Your home can be a great place to work, without your work taking over your home.

You start early, finish late and eat lunch at the keyboard. Your back aches, the laundry calls, messages arrive at 9 p.m. and some days you realize you have not spoken to another adult in person since the weekend.

Work From Home Made Simple is a practical, wry guide from a manager who ran remote teams across four time zones from a spare bedroom, a kitchen corner and a garden shed. You set up a comfortable workspace in any home, build routines that start and end the day, focus deeply, handle household distractions and children, communicate well, set boundaries and beat stress and isolation.
