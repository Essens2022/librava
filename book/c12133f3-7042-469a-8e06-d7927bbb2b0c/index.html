Tired of lying awake with a burning chest, living on boiled chicken and dry crackers? Your food can still taste good. It just needs to be gentler.

It is ten at night, dinner was at eight, and that slow burn is climbing behind your breastbone again while you sit bolt upright on the sofa. You have read the long lists of foods to avoid, cut out half the supermarket, and you still do not know what set it off this time.

Acid Reflux Cookbook Made Simple is written by a former restaurant line cook who lived with nightly heartburn for years. A food diary on her fridge showed that her real triggers were big late dinners, fried food and coffee, not everything on the scary lists, so she rebuilt her favorite dishes with gentler flavors. She shows you how to find your own triggers, which habits help many people most, and gives you 66 savory recipes with no chili heat, no frying, little or no tomato and garlic and onion always optional, so you can enjoy calmer days and nights alongside the care your doctor provides.
