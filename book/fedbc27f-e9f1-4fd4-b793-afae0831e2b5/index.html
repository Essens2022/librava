Learn Essential Hand Embroidery Stitches Step by Step and Create Beautiful Hoops, Gifts and Personalized Clothes, With 15 Easy Projects

Learn the gentle art of hand embroidery, one calm stitch at a time.

You have admired stitched hoops, flowery collars and personalized gifts and wished you could make them yourself. But tangled thread, confusing diagrams and too many supplies make it hard to know where to start.

Embroidery for Beginners Made Simple is a gentle, precise guide from a stitcher who learned from her grandmother and now teaches stitching circles. You set up your hoop and thread, learn 17 essential stitches with clear numbered steps, then make 15 easy projects, from your first hoops and flowers to names, clothes and handmade gifts.
