A feeder, a dish of water and a little know-how can bring a whole world of color and song to your window.

You hang a feeder and wait. A few birds come, then they vanish. You see a flash of red or a little bird upside down on a branch and wonder what it was. Bird books are full of hundreds of species you will never see in your yard.

Backyard Birds Made Easy focuses on the birds that actually visit homes in North America and the UK. You will learn what to feed, where to put feeders, how to add water and shelter, how to put up a nest box the right way, and how to identify your visitors by sight and sound in five simple steps.
