Everyday Habits to Help Keep Your Memory Sharp and Your Mind Active, With Easy Memory Techniques, a 30-Day Brain Health Starter Plan, a 4-Week Gentle Activity Plan, Brain Friendly Sleep and Eating Tips and Clear Advice on Normal Forgetfulness Versus Signs to Check With Your Doctor

Forgot a name again? Walked into a room and wondered why? You are not alone, and there is a lot you can do.

The word sits right on the tip of your tongue. Your glasses turn up on top of your head after ten minutes of searching. You laugh it off, but late at night a quiet worry creeps in: is this just getting older, or is it something more? Every headline pushes a new superfood, pill or brain game, and you are tired of guessing.

Brain Health After 50 Made Simple is a calm, reassuring and practical guide from a retired school librarian who started worrying about her own forgotten names and lost glasses, read everything sensible she could find and now leads a lively brain fit club at her local library, with walks, quizzes, learning and plenty of laughter. You learn how memory really works and why many slips are attention slips, then build everyday habits that are good for your whole body and may help your brain too: moving more, sleeping better, eating well, protecting your heart, hearing and vision, staying connected, learning new things and easing stress. Simple memory techniques and home routines make daily life easier right now, and a clear guide shows you which changes are normal and which are worth checking with your doctor, so you can feel more in control.
