Just been told you have type 2 diabetes or prediabetes? You do not have to give up proper food. You just need to balance it differently.

You came home from the appointment with a leaflet, a list of foods to avoid and a head full of worry. Now every meal feels like a test. Is bread allowed? What about rice, potatoes, pasta? You stare into the cupboard, the family is hungry and nobody explains it in plain words.

Type 2 Diabetes Cookbook Made Simple is written by a retired bus depot manager who was diagnosed at 52, panicked and threw out half his kitchen, then learned with his wife that he could still eat real food. Now he helps run a free cooking class for newly diagnosed neighbors. With no banned-food lists and no miracle diets, he shows you the plate method, sensible carb portions in hand measures, smart swaps and how to read a food label, then gives you 70 savory family recipes, each naming its carbohydrate food and a sensible portion, so you can feel more in control of every plate.
