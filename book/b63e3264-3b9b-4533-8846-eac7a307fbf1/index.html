72 Nourishing Savory Recipes That Are Soft, Moist and Easy to Chew for Recovery After Dental Work or Surgery, Chewing Difficulties, Dentures and Older Adults, With Smooth Blended Meals, Choking Safety Tips, a Two Week Meal Plan and Simple Ways to Add Protein When Appetite Is Small, With US and UK Measurements

Sore mouth, new dentures or fresh from the dentist's chair? You still deserve a proper dinner, not a bowl of beige mush.

Your teeth ache, your gums are tender or your dentures rub, and every meal turns into a battle. Toast scrapes, meat will not break down and plain mashed potato gets old by the second day. Maybe you are cooking for someone you love who pushes the plate away after three bites, and you worry they are not getting enough to eat. You want food that is safe, soft and kind to a sore mouth, but you also want it to taste like home.

The Easy Soft Foods Cookbook is written by a care home cook who spent nearly twenty years turning soft and blended diets into meals residents actually asked for, then put it all into practice at home when her husband Ray had his teeth out and her mother Edie moved in with new dentures and a tiny appetite. She shows you how to eat safely after dental work or surgery, how chewing problems differ from swallowing problems, how to keep small meals nourishing and how to make food soft without turning it to mush. Then she gives you 72 savory recipes, from creamy scrambled eggs and fish pie under silky mash to fork-tender beef stew, mild curry, soft lasagna and smooth blended roast dinners, so you can eat well and feel more in control again.
