You look after everyone. This book is a gentle reminder that you count too.

You are the one who remembers the birthdays, covers the extra shift and keeps the house running. You are tired in a way sleep does not fix, and self-care advice about spa days and expensive routines just makes you feel worse.

Self-Care Made Simple is a down-to-earth, gentle guide from a former hospital ward clerk and single mother of two who put everyone else first until she ran on empty. You learn small, affordable habits to rest without guilt, sleep better, care for your body on tired days, ease stress in the moment, talk to yourself kindly, set boundaries and build a plan that fits your real life.
