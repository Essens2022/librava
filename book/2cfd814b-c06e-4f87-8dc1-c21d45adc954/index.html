A salad should be the meal you look forward to, not the one you endure.

You want to eat more fresh food, but most salads leave you hungry an hour later. The lettuce wilts, the dressing comes from a bottle and by mid-afternoon you are raiding the snack drawer.

The Easy Salad Cookbook brings you 70 fresh, filling main-dish salads and dressings from a cook who ran a busy cafe salad counter for nine years with one rule: a salad must keep you full until dinner. You master the salad formula and make dressings, leafy mains, chicken, steak and seafood salads, grain and pasta bowls, bean and tofu salads and warm roasted salads.
