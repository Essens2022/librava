Composting Made Simple

Turn Kitchen and Garden Scraps Into Rich Soil in Any Space, With Easy Methods for Bins, Piles, Worms and Bokashi, Plus Fixes for Every Problem

Turn kitchen scraps and garden waste into rich, crumbly soil for free.

You hate throwing peelings and leaves in the trash, but composting sounds smelly, slow or complicated. Maybe you tried once and ended up with a soggy heap, fruit flies or a bin that never seemed to change.

Composting Made Simple is a down-to-earth guide from a community garden volunteer with twenty years of allotment experience. You learn how composting works, choose the right method for your space, get the mix of greens and browns right, build a bin or pile, try hot, slow, worm and bokashi composting, fix every common problem and use your compost well.
