Just been told your cholesterol is high? You do not have to live on bland diet food. You need a few smart swaps and dinners your family will actually eat.

The doctor said "your cholesterol is raised", and suddenly every meal feels like a mistake. You read one article blaming eggs and another blaming bread. You stand in the dairy aisle reading labels for forty minutes, come home with a carton of milk and still have no idea what to cook tonight.

Low Cholesterol Cookbook Made Simple is written by a former school catering manager who sat in the car park after her own diagnosis at 52, wondering what on earth she was supposed to eat now. With her doctor's blessing she spent two years quietly reworking her family's favorite dinners. She explains the four food levers linked to lower LDL cholesterol in plain words, shows you which swaps matter and how to read a label in seconds, then gives you 70 savory recipes built on oats, barley, beans, nuts and oily fish, so you can feel more in control of every plate alongside your doctor's advice.
