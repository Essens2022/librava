Write Your First Novel

A Step-by-Step Guide From Idea to Finished Draft. Find Your Story, Create Characters Readers Love, Plot With Confidence and Finish Your Book in 90 Days, Even If You Have Never Written Before

by Claire Bennett

€6.97 · Digital book (PDF)  |  €6.97 · eBook (EPUB)

You have a story in you. This book helps you get it onto the page and all the way to the end.

Maybe you have started a novel three times and stopped at chapter two. Maybe you have a notebook full of ideas and no idea where to begin. Most people who dream of writing a book never finish one, and it is rarely about talent.

Write Your First Novel walks you through the whole journey in plain, friendly words. Written by a former English teacher who wrote her own first novel in stolen hours at the kitchen table, it shows you how to choose a story idea, create characters readers care about, plan a plot that works, write lively scenes and dialogue, push through the messy middle and finish a complete first draft in 90 days.
