Rabbits are not starter pets. They are clever, social friends who deserve the right care.

You love your rabbit, but you are not sure you are getting it right. The pet shop said pellets and carrots were fine and a hutch was enough. Now your rabbit hides, chews everything and you worry you are missing signs that something is wrong.

The Happy Rabbit Handbook is a gentle, practical guide from a rescue volunteer who has fostered more than eighty rabbits. You learn to choose a healthy rabbit or pair, set up a safe home, feed the hay-first way, read body language, litter train, groom and trim nails, provide enrichment, bond rabbits, spot health problems early and care for an older rabbit with love.
