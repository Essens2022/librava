Own Less, Stress Less and Live More With a Gentle, Practical Plan to Clear Your Home, Calendar, Money and Mind, Without Throwing Away What Matters, With Room-by-Room Guides and 30-Day Challenges

Minimalism is not about empty white rooms. It is about having enough and room to breathe.

Every drawer is stuffed, the calendar is packed and money slips away on things you barely use. You feel guilty letting go, overwhelmed about where to start and tired of tidying the same piles every weekend.

Minimalism Made Simple is a calm, kind guide from a writer who once rented a storage unit for a house that was already too full, then downsized to a small apartment (flat) with her partner and two children. You find your reasons, shift your mindset, use a simple four-box method room by room, keep memories without the guilt, simplify as a family and bring the same calm to your spending, calendar and screens.
