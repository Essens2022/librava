Dog Separation Anxiety

A 21-Day Training Plan for Calmer Goodbyes: Leave Your Dog Home Alone With Less Barking, Panic and Chewed Furniture

by Jenna Marchbanks

€3.99 · eBook (EPUB)  |  €3.99 · Digital book (PDF)

Stop dreading the door. You pick up your keys and your dog is already panting. You check the camera at lunch and see the pacing, the drooling, the scratched door frame. A neighbor has left a note. This ebook gives you a calm, kind way out, built on the same approach veterinary and animal-welfare groups describe: tiny steps below the point where your dog gets upset, paid with food she loves, never with punishment.

What you get in 22 chapters:

A 21-day calendar: seven days of foundations at home, seven days from 1 second outside the door to 2 minutes, and seven days from 3 minutes to 45 minutes alone

An extension ladder from 60 minutes to 4 hours, with the percentage jump for every rung

A way to film your dog and find her exact threshold, plus green, amber and red body-language checklists

Scripts for greetings and goodbyes, for your manager, your neighbors and your dog walker

A vet-first chapter with the questions to ask, and a plain guide to trainers, behaviorists and veterinary behaviorists

Fixes that do not work, and what to use instead

Special cases: puppies, rescues, older dogs, noise fear, multi-dog homes

Ready-to-print log sheets, a video review sheet and a sitter sheet

Who it is for. Owners returning to the office, new rescue families, puppy owners who want to start right, and anyone whose dog cannot cope when the door closes.

An honest note. A 21-day plan is the shortest sensible path for a mild case. Many dogs need two to three months. This ebook is training information, not veterinary advice, and it gives no medication advice: it tells you when to see your vet first and when to ask for a specialist.
