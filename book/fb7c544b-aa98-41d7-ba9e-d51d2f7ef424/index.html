Creamy pasta, golden pies and proper comfort food, with not a drop of milk and nobody at the table missing out.

Maybe your stomach cramps after every milky coffee and you are tired of guessing. Maybe your child broke out in hives after one spoonful of yogurt and now every label feels like a threat. You find milk powder in the bread, whey in the crisps and casein in the "non-dairy" creamer, and dinner turns into plain chicken and a sad side of steamed vegetables while everyone else eats something creamy.

The Easy Dairy-Free Cookbook is written by a former café manager who blamed her stomach cramps on stress for years before a doctor confirmed lactose intolerance, and who later watched her toddler react to yogurt and get a milk allergy diagnosis. She learned that the two need very different care, so she now cooks one fully milk-free dinner for the whole family. She shows you how to tell intolerance from allergy, read labels in five countries, replace calcium on purpose and bring back creaminess with plant milks, olive oil, white beans, potatoes, cashews and nutritional yeast, then cook 73 savory recipes from mac and cheese and tikka masala to shepherd's pie, fish pie and party dips.
