Your first cruise should feel like a holiday, not a puzzle.

Cruise ads look dreamy, but the choices are dizzying. Which ship, which cabin, which route? You worry about hidden costs, seasickness, missing the ship in port and arriving with a suitcase full of the wrong clothes.

Cruise Travel Made Simple is a cheerful, honest guide from a former travel agent who has sailed on more than forty cruises of every size. You learn to choose the right type of cruise and itinerary, book at a fair price, pick a cabin you will be happy in, pack smart, glide through boarding day, enjoy life at sea and port days and avoid surprise charges.
