The Big Man's 16-Week Fat Loss Mission: Lose Up to 50 lbs Without Starving, Wrecking Your Joints or Living in the Gym

What if you could lose the weight without ever feeling starved again?

You've tried the tiny portions. The 1,200-calorie apps. The jumping workouts that wrecked your knees by Wednesday. And every time, hunger won. That's not because you're weak. It's because those plans were never built for a man your size.

Eat Big, Lose Big is. It's a 16-week fat loss mission designed for big men starting around 250–300 lbs who want to eat real food, train at home and finally see the scale move, week after week.

Here's what changes when you open this book:

You eat BIG plates. The Big Plate System fills your stomach with volume, so you stay full while the fat comes off. No apps, no weighing every bite.

12 big-portion recipes under 600 calories : smash burgers, steak and potatoes, giant burrito bowls, even pizza night. Plus a 14-day meal plan, a grocery list and a 2-hour Meal Prep Sunday plan.

16 joint-friendly exercises with photos. No gym, no jumping, no pain. Every move has Easier, Standard and Harder levels.

A daily plan for all 16 weeks. One page of orders and one page of proof for every week, so you never have to wonder what to do.

The Steps Ladder that takes you from 4,000 to 12,000 steps a day, one rung at a time.

The Calorie Ladder that adjusts your targets as you get lighter, so you keep losing.

Mind tools that kill cravings : the 10-Minute Rule, the Swish, the Identity Switch and a new mindset drill every week.

The Plateau Protocol and the "Never Miss Twice" rule , so one bad day never turns into a lost month.

Trackers, tests and a 16-week weight chart , plus the plan that takes you from 287 to 198 lbs (130 to 90 kg) after Week 16.

Why big men win with this plan:

Your size is not your problem. It's your secret weapon. A bigger body burns more calories, so you can eat more and still lose fast. Many men who start near 290 lbs and follow the plan lose 30 to 50 lbs in 16 weeks, and every pound you lose takes pressure off your knees with every step.

This book is for you if:

You're a man carrying 50 to 100+ extra pounds and you're done with diets that starve you

Your knees, back or schedule don't allow for gym classes or running

You want a simple plan you can follow every day without thinking

You want to feel strong, confident and in control again
