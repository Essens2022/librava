Grow Your Own Kitchen Herbs

An Easy Guide to Growing Fresh Herbs in Pots on Windowsills, Balconies and Patios, Plus Simple Ways to Harvest, Preserve and Cook With Them

by Clara Bennett

€4.97 · eBook (EPUB)

Fresh basil for your pasta, mint for your tea and rosemary for your roast, picked a few steps from your stove.

You buy a pot of supermarket basil and a week later it has flopped over and died. Fresh herbs at the store are expensive and wilt in the fridge. Yet a sunny windowsill or a small balcony is all you need to grow herbs that keep giving for months.

Grow Your Own Kitchen Herbs shows you how, even with no garden. You will learn which herbs suit your space, how to choose pots and compost, how much to water, how to harvest so plants grow bushier, how to keep them going through winter, and how to use and preserve your harvest in simple, delicious ways.
