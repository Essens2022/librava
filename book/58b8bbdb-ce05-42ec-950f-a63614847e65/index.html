Global Emergency 2026: The First 72 Hours

A Plan for Blackouts, Cyberattacks, War Alerts, Nuclear Incidents and Natural Disasters, With 34 Chapters, Printable Checklists and a Directory for 133 Countries

by Daniel Reyes Hart

€2.99 · Digital book (PDF)  |  €2.99 · eBook (EPUB)

War, cyberattacks, nuclear threats, blackouts: the world is on edge, and one day the lights, phones and payments can all stop at once. What do you do in the first hour, and how do you get through the next three days? This ebook gives you a calm, practical plan for the moment ordinary services stop working, written for ordinary households and built on what official civil-protection bodies actually say.

What is inside:

Why official guidance says to be ready to look after yourselves for at least 72 hours, what the European Commission published in 2026, and why some countries say more

How alerts reach you, what to do when the signal fails, and how to use emergency numbers

A family plan, a grab bag and a home kit explained by principle, with printable checklists to fill in

Water, food without power, medicines, documents, money, light, heat and communication

Hazard chapters that all follow the same order: warning signs, the first 60 seconds, what to do now, what not to do, shelter or evacuate, and the next 72 hours

Power cuts, water failure, heat, cold, floods, earthquakes, fires, storms and more, plus cyberattacks, pandemics, chemical incidents, war alerts and mass disruption, from a civilian point of view only

Extra planning for children, older adults, pets and life abroad, first aid basics and how to keep your head

A directory for 133 countries with emergency numbers, national authorities and alert systems where an official source was found, and guidance for every country

Who it is for. Families, people who live alone, parents, carers, travellers and anyone who would rather read a plan today than search for one in the dark.

An honest note. Where we could not confirm a number, a name or a procedure from an official source, we left it out or marked it Not independently verified. This ebook is general information, not medical, legal or engineering advice, and it never replaces the instructions of your emergency authority. Facts were checked in November 2026. First aid should be learned in a certified local course. People in the examples are invented.
