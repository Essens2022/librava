Everyone keeps asking you to take up their trousers. This book shows you how to turn that into a calm, professional home business.

You can already sew a straight seam and you have hemmed more trousers for family than you can count. Friends and neighbors keep asking for help, and you wonder whether you could charge for it. But the questions stop you. What should you charge? What if you ruin someone's good suit? What do you need to register, and are you even insured? And how do you stop a home full of other people's clothes turning into chaos?

Sewing Alterations Business Made Simple is a kind, precise and quietly funny guide from a former school dinner lady who learned to sew from her grandmother, started by hemming school trousers for the neighbors and has run an alterations and repairs service from her back bedroom for many years. She is a stickler for claim tickets and clear quotes, honest about the jobs she now turns down, and clear that regular clients take time to build. Step by step, she shows you how to set up your space, choose your services and limits, fit clients respectfully, hem, take in, replace zips and mend like a professional, price with a clear method, keep every job organized and handle busy seasons, mistakes and difficult clients calmly.
