Life After Divorce Made Simple

A Kind, Practical Guide to Healing After Separation, Handling Big Emotions, Telling the Children and Co-Parenting Calmly, Sorting Out Money, Housing and Paperwork, and Building a Happy New Life, With 8 Message Scripts, Ready-to-Use Checklists and a 30-Day Rebuilding Plan

Your marriage has ended. Your life has not. This is where the next chapter starts.

You lie awake replaying every conversation. There is a pile of paperwork you do not understand, a child asking why, an ex who knows exactly which buttons to press and a quiet house on the weekends that feels far too loud. Everyone has advice, and none of it tells you what to do tomorrow morning.

Life After Divorce Made Simple is a warm, steady companion from a former bank customer service team leader whose eighteen-year marriage ended suddenly, leaving her in a small rented flat with two children every other week and no idea where to start. She rebuilt step by step and now volunteers with a support group for separated parents. You learn how to get through the first weeks, ride the waves of grief and anger, tell the children kindly, co-parent calmly, take your first money, housing and paperwork steps, stay safe and build a new life that is truly yours.
