You already know your subject. This book shows you how to teach it, one student at a time, and run it as a real business.

Maybe you are a teacher who needs hours that fit around your own children, a graduate who loves explaining things, or a retiree with knowledge going to waste. But the questions stop you. Which subjects should you offer? Online or at your kitchen table? What checks do you need to work with children? What do you say to an anxious parent, what do you charge, and how do you find that first student?

Start a Tutoring Business Made Simple is a warm, organized guide from a former secondary (high) school math and science teacher who left the classroom when her children were small, started tutoring at her kitchen table and now teaches primary (elementary), exam-year and adult learners online and in person. She is honest about the slow first term, cancelled lessons and worried parents, and she shows you step by step how to choose your niche, set up legally and safely, plan lessons that work, track progress, keep parents calm, price with clear methods and grow at a pace that suits your life.
