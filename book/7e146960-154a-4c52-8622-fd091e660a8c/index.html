How to Buy a Used Car

The 40-Point Inspection Checklist, 15 Negotiation Scripts and the 9 Scams That Cost Buyers Thousands

by Dennis Aldridge

€3.99 · eBook (EPUB)  |  €3.99 · Digital book (PDF)

Your savings can vanish in one handshake. A rolled-back odometer. A loan nobody mentioned. A "deposit" sent to a stranger who never owned the car. It happens to careful people, and they usually find out after they have paid.

Now picture the other ending. You walk around the car with a list, you ask the questions the seller hoped you would skip, and you drive home knowing the deal is safe. Most bad used cars give themselves away in the first ten minutes, and you do not need to be a mechanic to see it. You need a list.

This ebook is that list, plus a budget that survives the first year, how to read an ad, what to ask before you drive, how to run a history check wherever you live, and how to close the deal without paying the wrong person.

What is inside

Forty numbered checks in six short rounds: paperwork, outside, inside, under the hood, test drive and closing

Fifteen word-for-word scripts for dealers and private sellers, including how to ask for the out-the-door price and how to say no to add-ons

Nine scams explained , from rolled-back odometers and flood cars to hidden dealers and fake escrow

Worked examples: a tabindex="-1"2,000 car that costs tabindex="-1"5,220 once you add what buyers forget

Six tear-out glove-box cards and a plan for your first thirty days

Who it is for. First-time buyers of any age, parents buying a car for a teenager, and anyone who has to get this right the first time.

An honest note. This is general information, not legal, financial or mechanical advice. Rights, fees and paperwork differ by country, state and province, so the ebook points to the official source to check.
