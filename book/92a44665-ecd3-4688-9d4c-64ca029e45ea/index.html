Plan, Book and Enjoy Your First Rail Adventure With Smart Routes, Rail Pass Tips and Ready-Made Itineraries From London to Rome

City center to city center, mountains out the window and no airport lines. Europe by train is easier than you think.

You love the idea of gliding from London to Paris, through the Swiss Alps and down to Rome. Then you start planning and the questions pile up. Pass or tickets? What is a seat reservation? Which station? What if you miss a connection? How much luggage can you bring?

Europe by Train Made Easy answers all of it in plain language. You get a calm, step-by-step system to choose your route, decide between a rail pass and point-to-point tickets, book on official sites, ride like a local and enjoy every minute. Seven ready-made itineraries make planning quick, from a first taste of Paris and Amsterdam to a full loop through the Alps and Italy.
