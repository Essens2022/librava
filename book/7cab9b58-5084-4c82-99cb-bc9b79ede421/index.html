Scared of your own dinner plate since the gallbladder trouble started? Light food can still be proper food, and you can feel more in control again.

You used to love a good dinner. Now every meal feels like a gamble. Maybe it was the attack at two in the morning after a rich meal, the endless wait for surgery, or the bloating and rushed trips to the bathroom since your gallbladder came out. The leaflet said "eat low fat" and nothing more, and the internet offers olive oil flushes and scary stories instead of a simple plan for tonight's tea.

The Easy Gallbladder Cookbook is written by a former school canteen manager who cooked for 400 children a day, then spent months with painful gallstone attacks and had her gallbladder removed by keyhole surgery at 49. She had to relearn her own cooking, and found that smaller plates, lighter cooking methods and bringing fat back slowly made all the difference. She explains your gallbladder in plain words, busts the flush and cleanse myths and gives you 70 gentle, savory recipes, a calm stage-by-stage plan for before and after surgery, a step-by-step fat reintroduction plan and a 28 day meal plan, all working alongside the care your doctor provides.
