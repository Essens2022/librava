Diagnosed with an underactive thyroid or Hashimoto's and suddenly scared of your own kitchen? You do not have to give up broccoli, bread or proper food.

You are cold when everyone else is comfortable, foggy by lunchtime and too tired to cook by evening. Then you go online and find endless lists of "thyroid toxic" foods that seem to ban half your shopping. Should you quit coffee? Is soy dangerous? Do you need kelp, or gluten-free everything? Nobody gives you a straight answer, and the family still needs feeding.

Hypothyroidism Cookbook Made Simple is written by a primary (elementary) school teacher who was diagnosed with Hashimoto's at 38 after two years of blaming her exhaustion on work. She spent months confused by scary food lists, then learned from her doctor and pharmacist that the real everyday wins were simple: take your tablet the same way every morning, eat regular meals and cook easy batches on good days for the tired ones. With calm, myth-busting answers and 70 savory family recipes, she helps you fit good food around your medicine and your energy levels and feel more in control of every meal, alongside the care your doctor provides.
