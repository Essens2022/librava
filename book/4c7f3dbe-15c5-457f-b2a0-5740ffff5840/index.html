Money for Couples Made Simple

How to Talk About Money Without Fighting, Build a Budget You Both Agree On, Tackle Debt Together and Plan for Shared Goals, With Conversation Scripts, Worksheets and a Step-by-Step Plan for Dating, Living Together and Married Couples

Money fights are not about money. They are about feeling heard. This book helps you both feel heard.

The same argument keeps coming back: who spent what, why the savings never grow, whose debt it really is. One of you worries, the other avoids, and talking about money feels like walking into a minefield.

Money for Couples Made Simple is a calm, fair guide from a former mortgage adviser who spent twelve years listening to couples argue across her desk and later trained in relationship communication. You understand your money personalities, talk without fighting, share the full picture kindly, choose how to split accounts and bills, build a budget you both agree on, tackle debt as a team and plan for shared goals.
