Camping for Beginners Made Easy

Everything You Need to Plan Your First Trip, Choose the Right Gear, Cook Great Food Outdoors and Sleep Warm and Safe Under the Stars

by Jake Morrison

€6.97 · Digital book (PDF)

Your first night under the stars should be warm, easy and fun, not a soggy story you tell for years.

Camping looks wonderful in pictures. Then you start planning and the questions pile up. Which tent? How cold is too cold? What do we eat? Where do we even go? And what if it rains?

Camping for Beginners Made Easy walks you through your first trip step by step, from choosing a campground or campsite and the right gear to setting up camp, cooking simple meals, building a safe campfire and sleeping warm. It is written for real beginners and families, with honest advice on what you need and what you can skip.
