If your heel feels like you are stepping on a tack every morning, you are not alone, and you are not stuck.

You sit on the edge of the bed bracing yourself, because the moment your heel touches the floor it stabs. You hobble to the kitchen like someone twice your age, ache through a long shift on hard floors and come home to a drawer full of insoles that promised a miracle and changed nothing. You are starting to wonder whether this will ever settle.

Plantar Fasciitis and Heel Pain Made Simple is a friendly, practical guide from a former retail store supervisor who spent years on hard shop floors, limped through a year of stabbing heel pain and finally learned from a podiatrist and a physical therapist that patience, stretching, strength and the right shoes work better than gadgets. She now leads a weekly walking group for people getting back on their feet. You learn what is really going on in your heel, protect your first steps, stretch and strengthen at your own pace, choose shoes that help and build your activity back with one simple rule, so you can feel more in control and support a steady recovery.
