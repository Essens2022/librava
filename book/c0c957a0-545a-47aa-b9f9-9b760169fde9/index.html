Lower bills without shivering on the sofa.

The bill lands and your stomach drops. You turn the heating down, put on another jumper and still the numbers climb. You hear advice everywhere but do not know which changes actually matter for your home.

Lower Your Energy Bills Made Simple is a practical, warm guide from a former home energy assessor who spent fifteen years crawling through attics (lofts) and basements. You learn to read your bill, start with free fixes, hunt down drafts, understand insulation, heat and cool smarter, cut hot water and appliance costs and decide which bigger upgrades are worth it, whether you rent or own.
