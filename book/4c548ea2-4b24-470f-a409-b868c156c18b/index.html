Eating well does not have to cost more. It just needs a plan and a few good recipes.

Food prices keep rising and healthy eating feels like a luxury. You buy vegetables with good intentions, throw half of them away and end up with takeout (takeaway) when you are tired, then wonder where the money went.

Healthy Eating on a Budget Cookbook brings you 70 easy, filling savory recipes from a mother who raised three kids on one modest income and taught cooking on a shoestring in a community food project. You plan meals, shop smart, batch cook and freeze and make hearty soups, bean dinners, thrifty meat dishes, pasta, tray bakes, packed lunches and use-it-up meals.
