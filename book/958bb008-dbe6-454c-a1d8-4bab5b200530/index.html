You are not losing your mind. You might be in perimenopause, and you deserve real help.

You wake drenched at 3 a.m., lose words mid-sentence and snap at the people you love over nothing. Your periods have gone haywire, your jeans no longer fit and you were told it is probably just stress. You are tired of scare stories, and even more tired of being told to just put up with it.

Menopause Made Simple is a frank, warm and funny guide from a former school office manager who spent a year in perimenopause without knowing it, until a friend marched her to the doctor. Now she hosts a monthly menopause chat group at her town library. She explains what is happening in plain words, gives you practical tools to try tonight for hot flashes, sleep, mood and brain fog, and prepares you to talk to your doctor about hormone therapy and other options so you leave with a real plan.
