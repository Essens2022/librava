Friends and neighbors keep calling you for shelves, sticking doors and dripping taps, and you wonder if it could be a real business. But the questions pile up. Do you need a license? Handyman Business Made Simple is a practical, plain-spoken guide from a former warehouse supervisor who spent weekends fixing things for family and neighbors and has run a one-van handyman business for many years.
