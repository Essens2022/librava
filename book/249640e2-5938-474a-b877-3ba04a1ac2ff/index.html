Fix It Yourself

The Beginner's Guide to Easy Home Repairs, From Leaky Faucets and Squeaky Doors to Patching Walls, That Save You Money and Calls to a Pro

by Dave Turner

€6.97 · Digital book (PDF)

Stop paying a pro for a five-minute fix. Learn to handle the small repairs yourself, safely.

The faucet drips, the bathroom door sticks, there is a hole in the wall where a shelf used to be and the toilet keeps running at night. None of it is hard. You just need the right tools, clear steps and someone to tell you what is safe to do yourself and what is not.

Fix It Yourself is that friendly guide. With a simple starter toolkit and easy, step-by-step instructions, you will fix more than 40 of the most common problems around the home, and learn exactly when to put the tools down and call a professional.
