Puppy Training for New Owners

A week-by-week plan for the first 12 weeks, with 36 fix-it cards, 8 command guides, 6 daily schedules, 10 checklists and a vet-visit prep sheet

by Kirsten Aldous

€3.99 · eBook (EPUB)  |  €3.99 · Digital book (PDF)

It is 2 a.m., the puppy is crying, the carpet is wet and you are typing "is this normal" into your phone.

Bringing home a puppy is joy and chaos in equal measure. Everyone tells you to be consistent, and almost nobody tells you what to actually do at the moment it gets hard. This ebook does. It gives you a calm daily rhythm, clear numbered actions and a plan for every week, all built on rewards, patience and good management.

The honest promise. Every method here is reward-based. There is no shouting, no nose-rubbing and no painful gear. It is educational, not veterinary advice, and it tells you plainly when to call your vet the same day and when to ask a qualified trainer for help.

What you get inside

A week-by-week plan for the first 12 weeks : one page for every week, with daily tick-box goals, new skills, socializing tasks, what to watch for and a Sunday check-in

36 fix-it cards : what you see, why it happens and three actions to try today, for accidents, nipping, whining, pulling, jumping, barking and more

8 command guides : name game, sit, down, come, wait, leave it, drop it and mat, each with numbered actions, common mistakes and a practice plan

6 daily schedules plus a blank one, from the first weeks at home to a rainy day indoors

10 checklists : puppy-proofing, shopping, arrival day, crate set-up, car trips, socialization, visitors, sitter handover, daily care and first-aid readiness

A vet-visit prep sheet , a household agreement, potty, sleep and training logs, a milestone tracker and weekly reviews

Glossary, 15 quick answers and an index , with line-art illustrations throughout
