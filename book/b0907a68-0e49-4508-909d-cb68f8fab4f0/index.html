You love children and you want to work from home. This book shows you how to turn that into a safe, legal business families trust.

Maybe you are at home with your own little ones and want an income that keeps you there. Maybe you are a nanny, a teaching assistant or an early years worker ready to run your own setting. But the questions pile up. Do you need a license? How many children can you take? What will the inspector look for? What do you write in a contract, what do you charge, and what do you say when a parent asks you to put their baby to sleep on her tummy or arrives late for the third time this week?

Home Daycare Business Made Simple is a calm, practical guide from a former preschool teaching assistant who became a registered home childminder when her own children were small and has cared for other families' children in her home for many years. She is cheerful about muddy play and glitter and completely uncompromising about safety, safeguarding and paperwork. Step by step, she shows you how to decide honestly if the work suits you, get licensed or registered, make every room safe, follow safe sleep and first aid rules, protect children, write contracts and policies, set fair fees, plan happy routines, guide behavior kindly, work in partnership with parents and fill your places.
