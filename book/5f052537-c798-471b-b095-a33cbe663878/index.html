Just told to try the low FODMAP diet for IBS? You do not have to live on plain rice. You can cook real dinners again.

Your dietitian or doctor handed you a list of foods to avoid, and suddenly onion and garlic are off the menu, wheat is limited and you are standing in front of the fridge with no idea what to make. Bloating, pain and unpredictable days have worn you down, the family is hungry and every label seems to hide something. You want relief, and you want food that still tastes like home.

Low FODMAP Cookbook Made Simple is written by a lifelong home cook who was diagnosed with IBS in her thirties, went through the diet with a dietitian and felt lost cooking family meals without onion and garlic, until she built a repertoire of normal-tasting dinners. Calm and organized, she explains IBS and FODMAPs in plain words, walks you through all three phases, shows you how to build flavor with garlic-infused oil, scallion and leek greens, chives, ginger and herbs, and gives you 66 savory recipes, each with a clear low FODMAP portion, so you can feel more in control and learn what your own gut handles.
