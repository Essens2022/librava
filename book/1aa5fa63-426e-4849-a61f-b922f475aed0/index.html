Strong After 40

The Simple Strength Training Plan for Women to Build Muscle, Protect Bones, Boost Energy and Feel Confident Through Midlife and Beyond

by Kate Morgan

€6.97 · Digital book (PDF)

Stronger legs, steadier balance, more energy and confidence. It is not too late. It might be the best time to start.

After 40, many women notice changes: less energy, aching joints, a body that feels different, and worries about bones and balance. Endless cardio and dieting rarely help. What does help, at any age, is getting stronger.

Strong After 40 is a friendly, step-by-step strength plan for women in midlife. Learn six key movements with clear form cues and easier or harder versions, then follow a 12-week plan at home or in the gym, just two or three short sessions a week. You will also learn how to eat, rest and recover to support your strength through perimenopause, menopause and beyond.
