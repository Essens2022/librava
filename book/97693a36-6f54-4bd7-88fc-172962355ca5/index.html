It is 3:07 a.m., your body is tired, and your mind has just opened a meeting with itself.

Lying awake is bad enough. Lying awake while you calculate how little sleep you will get, rehearse tomorrow and wonder whether something is wrong with you is what keeps the night going. This ebook gives you a calm, practical way to deal with the worry, not just the clock.

The honest promise. You will not find a cure here, and no serious ebook can offer one. You will find simple, non-clinical tools to make bad nights less frightening, settle faster after a wake-up and feel at home in your bedroom again. It is educational, not medical advice, and it tells you plainly when to speak to a doctor.
