You already know how to clean. This book shows you how to turn it into a real business.

Maybe you are already cleaning for a few neighbors for cash and wondering how to go professional. Maybe you need work that fits around school pickups, or a fresh start after a layoff. But how do you price a job, what insurance do you need, and how do you find clients who stay?

Start Your Own Cleaning Business is a straight-talking, step-by-step guide from an owner who started with a bucket, a borrowed vacuum (hoover) and three neighbors after a factory closed, and built a trusted local team over fifteen years. She is honest about her mistakes, from underquoting to a lost key. You choose your services, set up legally, protect your business, build your kit, work safely, price with confidence, win your first clients, then schedule, hire and grow.
