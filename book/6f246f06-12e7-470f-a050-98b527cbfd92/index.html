Grant Writing Made Simple

A Beginner's Guide to Finding the Right Funders, Reading Guidelines, Writing Strong Proposals, Needs Statements and Budgets, Measuring Outcomes and Reporting With Integrity, With 8 Ready-to-Use Templates and Checklists, 7 Proposal Section Templates and Funder Call Scripts for Nonprofits, Charities, Community Groups and Freelance Grant Writers

Your group does good work. Now learn to explain it so a funder can say yes.

You have a project that could change lives, but the funding forms feel like a foreign language. Eligibility rules, logic models, budget narratives, character limits and deadlines in someone else's time zone. You spend evenings writing, press submit, wait for months and get a polite no with no explanation, and you start to wonder whether grants are only for big organizations with professional fundraisers.

Grant Writing Made Simple is a calm, organized and honest guide from a former school office manager who volunteered to write a small grant for her town's food bank, was turned down twice, picked up the phone to ask why, and has since written proposals for youth clubs, community gardens, arts groups and small charities, first as a volunteer and later as a freelancer. She walks you through the whole grant cycle step by step: getting grant ready, finding funders who truly fit, reading guidelines, planning the project, writing a needs statement, outcomes and a budget that matches your story, submitting early, handling every decision and reporting with integrity, plus how to offer grant writing as an ethical paid service.
