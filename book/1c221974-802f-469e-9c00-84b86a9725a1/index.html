You could be great at helping people find home. You just want the honest version before you spend money on a license.

Maybe you are tired of a job that no longer fits, your children are older, or you want a second career that uses everything life has taught you. But the licensing rules seem different everywhere, every brokerage promises big earnings, and you have heard the stories about new agents who burned through their savings and quit within a year.

Become a Real Estate Agent Made Simple is a warm, candid guide from a former school administrator who got her license in her forties, nearly quit in her first lean year and went on to spend fifteen years helping first-time buyers and downsizing sellers before mentoring new agents. Step by step you learn what the job really involves, how licensing works where you live, how to pass the exam, how to choose a brokerage that teaches you, how to find your first clients among people you already know and how to guide buyers and sellers from first meeting to closing or completion. Then you learn to stay ethical, safe and solvent on irregular pay and follow a clear first-year plan.
