A Beginner's Guide to Genealogy. Trace Your Ancestors Step by Step, Find Records Online and Offline, Use DNA Tests Wisely, Organize Your Research and Preserve Your Family Story for Future Generations

Somewhere in the records is a name, a date and a story that leads straight back to you.

You have a box of old photos with no names on the back, a grandparent's story you only half remember and a feeling that there is so much more to find. Family history can seem overwhelming, full of websites, records and unfamiliar words.

Discover Your Family History makes it simple. Written by a retired school librarian who started with a box of old letters and now helps beginners every week, it shows you how to start with what you know, interview relatives kindly, build an accurate family tree, find and read the key records in the US and UK, understand DNA tests and their limits, break through brick walls and preserve everything for the next generation.
