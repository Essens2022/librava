If you cannot touch your toes, this book was written for you.

Yoga can loosen a stiff back, steady your balance and calm a busy head, but most beginner classes move too fast and assume you already know the poses. This book starts where you are: at home, with a mat or a rug, a chair and a couple of thick books standing in for blocks.

Easy Yoga at Home for Beginners walks you through 40 gentle poses step by step, each with an easier option and a clear note on what to watch out for. Then it hands you ready-made routines of 5 to 30 minutes for mornings, desk stiffness, stress, better sleep and gentle strength, plus a 30-day plan to make it a habit.
