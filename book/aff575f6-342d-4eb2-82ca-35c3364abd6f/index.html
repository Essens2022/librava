Learn Your First Chords, Strum and Pick With Confidence and Play Real Songs in 30 Days, Even If You Have Never Held a Guitar Before

You do not need talent, a teacher or music reading to play your first real songs.

Maybe there is a guitar in the corner that you bought with big plans. Maybe you tried as a teenager, your fingertips hurt, the chords buzzed and you gave up. Most beginner books throw scales, theory and tiny diagrams at you, and the fun never arrives.

Guitar for Beginners Made Simple takes you step by step from holding the guitar to playing complete songs, with clear chord tables, easy strumming patterns you can count, and short daily practice that fits a busy life. Every chord, pattern and tip is explained in plain English by a patient teacher who has helped hundreds of adult beginners.
