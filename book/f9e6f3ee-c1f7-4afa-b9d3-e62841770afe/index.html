Learn to knit, one calm stitch at a time.

You have seen beautiful handmade scarves, hats and blankets and thought, I wish I could make that. Then you tried a video, the yarn tangled, the stitches fell off the needle and you gave up.

Knitting for Beginners Made Simple starts at the very beginning and explains every move in plain words: casting on, knit and purl, reading patterns, increases and decreases, knitting in the round and fixing mistakes. Then you put each skill to work in 12 cozy projects you will actually use.
