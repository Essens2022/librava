Fresh eggs for breakfast, happy hens in the garden and a hobby the whole family loves.

You have seen the photos: a pretty coop, a basket of brown eggs, a few friendly hens wandering the lawn. But how much space do they need? What about foxes, winter, holidays, the neighbors and the rules where you live?

Backyard Chickens for Beginners answers every question in a warm, honest, practical way. You will decide if chickens suit your life, check the rules, choose friendly breeds, set up a safe coop and run, raise chicks or start with grown hens, feed them properly, keep them healthy and handle your eggs safely, season after season.
