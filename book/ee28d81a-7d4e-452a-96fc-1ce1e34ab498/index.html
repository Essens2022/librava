Fresh eggs for breakfast, happy hens in the garden and a hobby the whole family loves.

You have seen the photos: a pretty coop, a basket of brown eggs, a few friendly hens wandering the lawn. But how much space do they need? What about foxes, winter, holidays, the neighbors and the rules where you live?
