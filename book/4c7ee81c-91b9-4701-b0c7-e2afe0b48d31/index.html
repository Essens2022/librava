You do not need talent to start painting. You need a brush, a few tubes and a friendly guide.

You have always wanted to paint, but a blank canvas feels terrifying. The colors turn to mud, the paint dries before you can blend it and a quiet voice tells you that you are just not artistic.

Acrylic Painting for Beginners Made Simple is a warm, encouraging course from a former theater set painter who now runs relaxed evening painting classes. You build a small starter kit, learn to mix any color from a limited palette, practice twelve simple brush marks and then paint twelve complete pictures step by step, from glowing sunsets and lemons on a cloth to rolling waves, sunflowers and a starry galaxy.
