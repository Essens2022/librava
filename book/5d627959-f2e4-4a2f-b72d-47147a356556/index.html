You tried keto once and lived on bacon and cheese. Here is the version that actually feels good.

You keep hearing about keto, but the internet is a mess of macros, fat bombs and arguments. You are not sure what net carbs are, why your friend felt awful in week one, or whether keto is even safe with your health and your medicines. And you still have to put a normal dinner on the table for a family who loves potatoes.

The Easy Keto Cookbook for Beginners is written by a former office manager and busy mother of two who tried keto three times. The first two attempts were bacon, cheese, headaches and giving up by Friday. The third time she talked to her doctor first, salted her food, filled half her plate with vegetables and cooked meals her husband and kids would eat too. She shares 70 simple savory recipes built on vegetables, olive oil, fish, eggs, poultry, nuts and cheese, each with an approximate net carb band, plus plain, honest guidance on starting safely, getting through keto flu and choosing to carry on, ease off or move to moderate low carb.
