You can already tell when words fall flat. This book teaches you to write the ones that work.

You read a local website that says "quality products, passionate service" and think, I could do better than that. But when you sit down to write, the words come out stiff, salesy or vague. You have heard copywriting can be a real career, yet you have no portfolio, no idea what to charge or how to find a single client, and every course you find shouts about overnight riches and fake countdown timers.

Copywriting for Beginners Made Simple is a witty, direct and generous guide from a freelance copywriter who started writing product descriptions in the back office of a mail order catalog, under a manager who circled every lazy adjective in red. After agency work and eleven years writing for local shops, charities, tradespeople and online businesses, she shows you the whole craft: research your reader, turn features into benefits, write headlines that get read, structure a persuasive message, edit for clarity and keep every claim honest. Then she walks you from zero samples to a portfolio, a proper business setup and your first client conversations, with no hype and no income promises.
