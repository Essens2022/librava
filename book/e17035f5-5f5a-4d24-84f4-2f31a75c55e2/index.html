The lunchbox comes home at 3:30 with the sandwich still inside, one tiny bite out of the corner. It does not have to be like this.

You get up early, you pack it with love and you send it off hoping. By afternoon it is back, sweaty and untouched, and you are left wondering whether your child ate anything at all. Dinner is no easier. One night the pasta is perfect, the next it is "too pasta." Grandma says just make them eat it, school says no nuts, and you are tired of cooking food that ends up in the bin.

The Easy Lunchbox Cookbook for Picky Eaters is written by a mum of one famously fussy son and one daughter who will eat anything, who spent years as a school lunchtime supervisor watching thousands of lunchboxes get opened, ignored and swapped. She knows what children actually eat, and she is on your side and your child's side at once. She shows you the calm, no-pressure way to feed a picky eater, a simple five-part formula for a lunchbox your child will open, how to keep packed food safe and how to cut food for little ones, then gives you 65 savory, nut-free recipes, from pinwheels and pizza lunchboxes to warm flask lunches, freezer muffins and family dinners that turn into tomorrow's lunch.
