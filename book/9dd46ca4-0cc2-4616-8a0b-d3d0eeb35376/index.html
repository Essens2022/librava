Baking feels risky because it is exact, but it only asks for precision in a few places. Once you know where, every recipe becomes repeatable. This book shows you where, then hands you more than 70 dependable recipes with cups and grams, honest times and clear signs for when each bake is done.

Easy Everyday Baking is written for beginners and busy bakers in US and UK kitchens, using supermarket ingredients and pans you already own. Every recipe gives both ingredient names where they differ, so flour, sugar and chocolate mean the same thing on both sides of the Atlantic.
