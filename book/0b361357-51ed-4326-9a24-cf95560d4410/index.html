Woke up with a big toe so painful even the bedsheet hurt? You do not have to give up good food. You need to know what to eat tonight.

The doctor said "gout", and now every meal feels like a trap. One website bans spinach, another blames tomatoes, a third swears a juice will "flush it out". You stand at the fridge wondering if anything in it is safe, while the memory of that last flare makes you scared to eat at all.

Gout Diet Cookbook Made Simple is written by a retired bus depot supervisor who woke at 54 with his big toe on fire and a diet of beer, steak and sugary soda that was not helping. With his doctor's treatment and his wife's teasing, he learned to cook. He explains uric acid, purines and medicines in plain words, shows why food helps but is often not enough on its own, sorts foods and drinks into enjoy often, moderation and limit, then gives you 72 savory lower purine recipes built on vegetables, beans, eggs, low-fat dairy and grains, with sensible portions of chicken and fish, so you can eat well, without guilt, and feel more in control alongside your treatment.
