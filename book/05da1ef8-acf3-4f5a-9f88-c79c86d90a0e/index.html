Crackly crust, soft open crumb and that wonderful tang, baked in your own kitchen.

You have seen the beautiful loaves online and maybe tried once or twice. The starter never seemed to rise, the dough spread like a pancake or the inside was gummy. The good news is that sourdough is not difficult. It just needs a clear method, accurate weights and a few dough signs you learn to read.

Sourdough Made Simple takes you from flour and water to your first proud loaf, step by step. Build a lively starter, bake a reliable beginner loaf with a full timeline, learn to shape and score, fix common problems and fit baking into a busy week. Then keep going with everyday breads, discard recipes and sweet bakes.
