Home from the hospital with a leaflet that says "clear liquids, then low fiber" and no idea what to cook? This book tells you, meal by meal.

The pain scared you, the diagnosis confused you and now every meal feels like a gamble. One person says never eat seeds, another says eat more fiber, and the leaflet stops at a single line. You stand at the open fridge wondering what is safe today, what is safe next week and whether you will ever enjoy a proper dinner, a bowl of popcorn or a handful of nuts again.

Diverticulitis Cookbook Made Simple is written by a retired bus driver and lifelong home cook who ended up in hospital with diverticulitis at 56 and came home with no idea what to eat. With his wife Maureen beside him at the stove, he spent the next year learning what works. He explains diverticulosis, diverticular disease and diverticulitis in plain words, walks you through a clear three stage flare plan that sits alongside your doctor's advice, shows you how to add fiber gently without wind and bloating, then gives you 69 savory recipes, from golden clear broths for the hard days to chili, curries, pilafs and a proper lentil shepherd's pie for the good ones, so you can eat well and feel more in control again.
