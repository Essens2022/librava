Came home from the kidney clinic with a food list and thought, so what am I supposed to eat now? This book answers that, one tasty dinner at a time.

Half your favorite foods seem to be on the wrong side of the page. Salt is out, potatoes need special treatment, phosphorus hides in packets and nobody has time to explain it slowly. Whether you live with kidney disease or cook for someone who does, every shopping trip feels like a test you might fail.

Kidney Diet Cookbook Made Simple is written by a former home economics teacher who cooked for her father for eight years as his kidney disease moved to dialysis, sat in on every dietitian session with a notebook and now runs a cooking group for kidney patients and carers. She explains sodium, potassium, phosphorus, protein and fluids in plain words, shows you how to leach vegetables and spot phosphate additives, then gives you 70 savory recipes made without added salt or processed meats, each ending with a Kidney note to talk through with your renal dietitian, so you can feel more in control of every meal.
