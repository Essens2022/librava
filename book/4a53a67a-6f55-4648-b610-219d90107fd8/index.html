You take great photos. People keep asking you to shoot for them. Now it is time to get paid properly, without the expensive mistakes.

Maybe friends already ask you to photograph their kids, their weddings or their small business products, and you wonder whether you could charge for it. But the questions pile up. What gear do you really need? What do you charge without scaring people off or working for nothing? What goes in a contract, who owns the photos, and what happens if a memory card fails in the middle of someone's wedding? You do not want to learn all of that the hard way, on a client who trusted you with a day they can never repeat.

Start a Photography Business Made Simple is a relaxed, honest guide from a former insurance claims handler who photographed friends' weddings and kids' soccer (football) matches for years, went part-time, then full-time, and has run a small portrait, event and product photography business ever since. He is funny about his early mistakes, including the wedding where a card failed and the second card saved him, and strict about contracts, backups and the boring business work that keeps clients safe. Step by step, he shows you how to choose your services, buy gear sensibly, set up insurance and contracts, build a portfolio that wins bookings, price from your real costs and time, give clients a great experience, edit faster, protect every file and find clients who book, refer and return.
