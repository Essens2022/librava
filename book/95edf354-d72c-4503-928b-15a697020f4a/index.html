One deck of cards. Forty games. A lifetime of laughter around the table.

Everyone is on a screen, family time feels flat and you cannot remember the rules to the games you played as a child. When you try to teach one, an argument breaks out about whether aces are high.

Card Games for the Whole Family is a warm, patient guide from a retired school caretaker and grandfather of seven who runs a weekly games afternoon at his local library. You learn card basics in ten minutes and then forty classic games with clear rules, ages, player counts and scoring, from War, Snap and Go Fish to Rummy, Whist, Hearts, Cribbage, Spoons and Klondike.
