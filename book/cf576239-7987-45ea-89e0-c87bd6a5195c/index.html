You catch your reflection in a shop window and see a stoop you do not recognize. Good news: you do not need a perfect posture. You need this simple plan.

You have tried to sit up straight a hundred times. You pull your shoulders back at your desk, slump again ten minutes later and stand up after a long day feeling like a rusty folding chair. Your neck feels heavy after scrolling, your hips are tight after driving, and every photo seems to show your head pushed a little further forward. Braces, reminders and willpower have only left you more tense and more frustrated.

Better Posture Made Simple is an upbeat, myth-busting guide from a former bus driver who spent twenty years in one seat until old family photos showed he was stooping just like his grandfather. A physical therapist taught him that moving often and building strength beat holding one rigid position, and he now runs a free move more, sit less stretch session for office workers and retirees. You learn why slouching is not damage and why your best posture is your next one, then get movement breaks that fit any day, a comfortable chair, car and desk setup, easy phone neck fixes, 7 opening stretches, 9 strength moves and gentle help for older adults, so you can feel more comfortable, move more freely and stand taller with confidence.
