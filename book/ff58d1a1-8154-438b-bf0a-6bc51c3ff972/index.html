Woodworking for Beginners Made Simple

Learn the Tools, Joints and Finishes Step by Step and Build 12 Useful Projects for Your Home, Even With a Small Space and a Basic Toolkit

by Dan Whitaker

€4.97 · eBook (EPUB)

You do not need a big workshop to build beautiful, useful things.

You have seen the shelves, benches and cutting boards people make in their garages and thought, I would love to do that. Then the questions start: which tools, which wood, how do the pieces stay together, and how do you keep all ten fingers?

Woodworking for Beginners Made Simple starts from zero and takes you there one skill at a time. You learn to work safely, set up a small space, choose a sensible starter toolkit, measure and cut straight, drill, glue, sand and finish, and make joints that hold. Then you put each skill to work in 12 step-by-step projects with clear cut lists in inches and millimeters.

The 12 projects

Coasters, a cutting board, a wall shelf, a step stool, a storage crate, a planter box, a picture frame, a bookshelf, a side table, a shoe rack bench, a garden bench and your own workbench.

Every project teaches one new skill, so each build is a little better than the last.
