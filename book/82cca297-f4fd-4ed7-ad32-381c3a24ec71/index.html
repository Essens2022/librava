Toast for breakfast, a sad salad for lunch and hungry again by nine? Vegan food can be so much better than that.

Maybe you have just decided to go vegan, or your teenager has, and suddenly every meal feels like a puzzle. You leave the meat off the plate and wonder why you are starving an hour later. Tofu turns out pale and soggy. You worry about protein and B12, find milk powder in your favorite crisps (chips) and have no idea what to cook for a family who still eats meat.

Vegan Cooking for Beginners Made Simple is written by a former school librarian who went vegan at 38 when her fifteen-year-old son announced he was done with meat and she refused to cook two dinners every night. Her first month was toast, sad salads and badly cooked tofu, until she learned to build meals around beans, lentils and tofu properly. Now she runs a monthly plant-based potluck at her local library and still cooks for a cheerful meat-eating husband. With no guilt and no miracle claims, she gives you 69 filling savory recipes, a plain guide to the nutrients vegans must plan for, a beginner pantry, label reading help and a gentle four week plan that changes one meal at a time.
