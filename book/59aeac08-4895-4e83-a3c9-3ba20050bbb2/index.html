Puddles on the floor, needle teeth on your hands and crying all night? It gets better, fast.

Bringing home a puppy is pure joy, until the first night. Then come the accidents, the nipping, the chewed shoes and a pile of advice from the internet that all says something different. You just want a clear plan that is kind, simple and actually works.

The Happy Puppy Plan gives you exactly that. Using gentle, reward-based training in sessions of just a few minutes a day, you will teach the basics, prevent most problems before they start and raise a calm, confident and friendly dog. A day-by-day 30-day plan ties everything together, so you always know what to practice next.
