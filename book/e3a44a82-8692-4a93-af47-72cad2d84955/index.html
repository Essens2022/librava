Help Your Child Handle Big Feelings, Bounce Back From Setbacks and Solve Problems With Confidence, With Everyday Scripts, Games and Calm Strategies for Ages 4 to 12 and Clear Advice on When to Seek Extra Help

Give your child the inner strength to face hard days and bounce back.

Small setbacks turn into big meltdowns. Your child worries, gives up quickly or melts down over mistakes, and you wonder how to help without fixing everything or making it worse. You want to protect them, but you know you cannot shield them from every hard day.

Raising Resilient Kids Made Simple is a wise, warm guide from a former primary school teacher and pastoral lead of twenty years who is now a grandmother of four. You build a strong connection, help your child name and tame big feelings, handle anger and worry, solve problems, learn from mistakes, cope with friendships and big changes and know exactly when to seek extra help.
