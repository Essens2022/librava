You are not lazy, broken or careless. Your brain just needs systems built for the way it works.

Late again, despite leaving early. Keys missing, a bill overdue, three planners abandoned and a pile on the chair that has quietly become furniture. You know exactly what to do and still cannot start, and every "just try harder" makes you feel worse.

Adult ADHD Organizing Made Simple is a warm, funny, shame-free guide from a former events coordinator who could rescue a wedding at the last minute but not pay her own electric bill on time. Diagnosed in her late thirties, she now runs a peer support group for adults with ADHD. You learn simple, visible, forgiving systems for time, planning, starting tasks, routines, home, money, work, relationships, big feelings and focus, so daily life feels lighter and more in your control.
