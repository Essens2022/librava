Gentle Step by Step Ways to Beat Shyness, Feel Calmer Around People, Start and Keep Conversations Going, Quiet Your Inner Critic and Enjoy Work, Parties and Everyday Social Life, With Fear Ladders, Conversation Scripts, a Calm-Down Card and a 30-Day Practice Plan

You do not have to become loud or outgoing. You just need fear to stop making your choices for you.

Your heart pounds before a phone call. You rehearse every word before ordering a coffee, go red when someone looks your way and stay silent in meetings with a good idea on your tongue. You turn down the party, feel relief, then spend the evening replaying every awkward moment and wondering why everyone else finds this so easy.

Overcoming Social Anxiety Made Simple is a kind, quietly funny guide from a former bookshop assistant who let the phone ring out for twenty years, hid in the stockroom when visitors came and went the color of a ripe tomato at every unexpected question. Through small daily steps she built a fuller social life and now hosts a relaxed small talk practice meetup. You learn how the anxiety cycle works, calm your body, catch thinking traps, turn your attention outward, drop the hidden props that feed fear and climb your own fear ladder, then use those tools for conversations, calls, shops, work, study, parties and friendships, so you can feel calmer and do more of what you really want.
