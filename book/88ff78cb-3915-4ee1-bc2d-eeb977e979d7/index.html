Calm, Practical Steps to Protect Your Home and Loved Ones in Power Outages, Storms, Floods, Fires and Other Emergencies, for the US and UK

Emergencies are stressful. Being ready for them does not have to be.

The power goes out for two days. A storm closes the roads. A flood warning arrives at midnight. In the moment, the families who cope best are not the ones with bunkers and stockpiles. They are the ones who took a few calm, simple steps in advance.

Ready for Anything is a reassuring, practical guide for families, couples, older adults and people living alone in the US and UK. Step by step, you will learn your local risks, make a family plan, build a home kit and grab-and-go bags, store water and food safely and know exactly what to do in the most common emergencies.
