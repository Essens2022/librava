Downsize Without the Drama

A Calm, Room-by-Room Plan to Declutter, Let Go With Less Guilt and Move Into a Smaller Home You Love

by Margaret Ellison

€4.97 · eBook (EPUB)

Thirty years of belongings, one smaller home and no idea where to start? Start here.

You open a closet, see decades of memories looking back at you and quietly close the door again. You worry about what to keep, what the children will want, what anything is worth and how on earth it will all be done before moving day. You are not alone, and you do not have to do it all at once.

Downsize Without the Drama is a calm, kind and practical plan for empty nesters, new retirees and adult children helping a parent move. It shows you how to sort a whole house one small step at a time, talk to your family without arguments, let go of things with less guilt and arrive in your new home with only what you love and use.

12, 6 and 3 month timelines

You are not getting rid of your life. You are choosing the pieces of it that come with you.
