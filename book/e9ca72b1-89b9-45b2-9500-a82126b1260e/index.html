Before you buy another gadget, do the math. A smart bulb promises a lighter power bill, a plug promises to kill standby waste, a thermostat promises hundreds back. Some keep the promise, many do not. This ebook gives you a simple method to tell the difference before you spend, using your own watts, your own bill and a pencil.

What you get in 20 chapters:

The payback method in three lines, with worked sums you can repeat for any gadget

Nine full calculations: TV corner, desk, water heater, washer, thermostat, radiator valves, IR remote, old fridge and cameras, each with a verdict

How to read your bill and measure standby power with a 15-dollar watt meter

Wi-Fi, voice assistants, Matter, Thread and Zigbee explained in plain words

A leak-sensor threshold: when 60 dollars of sensors beat a 1,500-dollar flood

Camera costs over five years and privacy rules of good behavior

A table of what renters can usually install without asking

Seven gadgets not to buy, and the five questions that filter any purchase

A 100-dollar starter kit and a 30-day plan, plus a troubleshooting table for when it stops working

Who it is for. Homeowners, renters and families who want a smarter home without wasting money or time, and who prefer a number to a sales pitch.

An honest note. All prices, tariffs, plug types and voltages differ by place, so the examples use US dollars, an illustrative 0.20 dollars per kWh and clear assumptions, and show you how to check yours. This ebook is general information, not electrical, safety or financial advice. People in the examples are invented.
