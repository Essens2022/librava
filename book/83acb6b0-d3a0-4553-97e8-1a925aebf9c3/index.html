You have not failed at quitting. You just never had a proper plan.

You have quit before. Maybe for a day, maybe for three weeks. Then came the party, the bad day at work or the drink with friends, and suddenly you were bumming a cigarette or reaching for the vape again, telling yourself you just do not have the willpower. Or you switched to vaping to save your lungs and now you cannot put the thing down for a whole film.

Quit Smoking and Vaping Made Simple is a warm, no-lecture plan from a former warehouse supervisor who smoked for twenty years, quit more times than he can count, switched to a vape and then had to quit that too. Now he volunteers at a community stop smoking group. He shows you how to tackle the nicotine, the habit and the feelings all at once: map your pattern, pick the right date and method, line up free support, use nicotine replacement or other quit aids properly, get through quit day and the hard first weeks, and get straight back on track after a slip, so this attempt has a much better chance of being the one that sticks.
