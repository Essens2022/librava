You can teach your children at home. You do not have to be a teacher to do it well.

You are thinking about homeschooling, or you have just started, and the questions keep coming. Is it legal where you live? What do you teach, and how? Will your children make friends? Will you burn out by Christmas?

Homeschooling Made Simple is a reassuring, organized guide from a former elementary (primary) teacher who homeschooled her three children through a cross-country move and a stint abroad. You check the rules, choose an approach, plan a year without overplanning, build a daily rhythm, teach core subjects, keep simple records, support every kind of learner and protect your family from burnout.
