Fishing for Beginners Made Simple

How to Choose Your Gear, Tie the Right Knots, Find the Fish and Catch Your First Bass, Trout and Panfish From Shore or Boat

That first tug on the line is a feeling you never forget. This book gets you there.

The tackle aisle is overwhelming. Hundreds of lures, dozens of rods, line in every color and nobody to explain which ones you actually need. So you buy a cheap kit, sit by the water for an afternoon and go home with nothing but a tangle.

Fishing for Beginners Made Simple shows you exactly what to buy, how to set it up, which five knots to learn, what bait and lures to use and where fish hide in ponds, lakes and rivers. Step-by-step chapters cover the most popular freshwater fish, from panfish and bass to trout and catfish, plus a section for anglers in the UK.
