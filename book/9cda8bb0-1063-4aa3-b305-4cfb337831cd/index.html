Easy Spanish for Travelers

The Phrases, Pronunciation and Confidence You Need to Order Food, Get Around, Make Friends and Handle Any Situation in Spain and Latin America

by Sofia Navarro

€6.97 · Digital book (PDF)

Speak a little Spanish and every trip feels warmer, easier and a lot more fun.

You smile, point at the menu and hope for the best. The taxi driver asks something you do not understand. The waiter is waiting. A few simple words would change everything, but most phrasebooks are long lists with no idea how to actually say them.

Easy Spanish for Travelers gives you the phrases you will really use in Spain, Mexico and the rest of Latin America, each with a simple sound guide so you can say it with confidence the first time. Short dialogues show how real conversations go, and clear notes explain what changes from country to country.
