Real Mexican flavor at home, without a single hard-to-find ingredient.

You love the tacos, salsas and enchiladas from your favorite restaurant, but at home everything tastes flat. Recipes call for chiles you cannot find and take all afternoon, so you end up reaching for a packet kit again.

The Easy Mexican Kitchen Cookbook brings you 70 bold, simple recipes from a cook who grew up beside her grandmother in a border town and worked in a busy taqueria kitchen. You make fresh salsas and guacamole, tacos for every night, enchiladas and burritos, pozole and tortilla soup, rice and beans, fajitas, seafood, vegetarian mains and weekend brunch with supermarket ingredients.
