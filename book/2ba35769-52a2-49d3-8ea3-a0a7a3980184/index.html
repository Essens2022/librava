The 28-Day Partner Workout Challenge to Build Strength, Burn Fat & Get Fit Together at Home — No-Equipment Bodyweight Training for Men and Women

What if the secret to finally getting fit wasn't more willpower… but the person sitting next to you?

Most workout plans fail for one simple reason: you do them alone. No one to push you. No one to notice when you skip. No one to celebrate the wins. This book changes that.

Military Calisthenics for Couples takes the proven discipline of military bodyweight training and turns it into a 28-day mission you and your partner complete together — at home, with zero equipment, in as little as 15–30 minutes a day.

No gym membership. No complicated routines. No guessing. Just two people, one plan, and real progress you can see and feel.

Inside, you'll discover:

A complete 28-day military-style program — one page per day, every mission mapped out

20 exercises with full-color photos — 12 side-by-side moves and 8 true partner drills like the High-Five Push-Up, Back-to-Back Squat and Mirror Lunge

Three levels for every move — Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced — so you can train together even at different fitness levels

15–30 minute workouts that fit into the busiest schedules

Joint-friendly options and a clear safety guide

A Day 0 and Day 28 fitness test so you can measure exactly how far you've come

Daily mission logs, weekly check-ins and before-and-after trackers to keep you both accountable

Simple nutrition for two , a shared grocery list and recovery tips

A 90-day continuation plan and 10-minute emergency workouts for busy days

The Couple's Pact and a Certificate of Completion to start strong and finish proud

Why couples who train together win:

When you commit together, you show up more often, push harder and stay consistent longer. Along the way, you build more than muscle — you build trust, teamwork and healthy habits that spread through your whole home.

Couples who want to lose weight and get stronger together
