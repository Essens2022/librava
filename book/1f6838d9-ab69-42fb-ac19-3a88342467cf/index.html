Speak a little Italian and every trip feels warmer, easier and a lot more fun.

You smile, point at the menu and hope for the best. You ask for a latte and get a glass of milk. The train leaves from a platform you cannot find. A few simple words would change everything, but most phrasebooks are long lists with no idea how to actually say them.

Easy Italian for Travelers gives you the phrases you will really use in Italy, each with a simple sound guide so you can say it with confidence the first time. Short dialogues show how real conversations go, and clear notes explain coffee bars, menus, trains and the customs that make you a welcome guest.
