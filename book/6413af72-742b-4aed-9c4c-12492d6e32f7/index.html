People already ask you to fix things. Here is how to get paid properly and safely.

Friends and neighbors keep calling you for shelves, sticking doors and dripping taps, and you wonder if it could be a real business. But the questions pile up. Do you need a license? What if you drill into a pipe? Which jobs could land you in legal trouble? And how do you quote without working all afternoon for an hour's pay?

Handyman Business Made Simple is a practical, plain-spoken guide from a former warehouse supervisor who spent weekends fixing things for family and neighbors and has run a one-van handyman business for many years. It teaches the business, not the repairs: which services to offer, which jobs to refer to licensed electricians, plumbers and gas engineers, how to check local rules and get insured, how to work safely, quote with confidence, put it in writing, find customers, give great service and grow at your own pace.
