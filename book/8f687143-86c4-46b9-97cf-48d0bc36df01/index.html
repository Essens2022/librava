Morning Routine for a Calm Start to Your Day

Three Mornings of 10, 30 and 60 Minutes, a 7-Minute Evening Reset, a 14-Day Trial and 12 Rituals to Choose From, Even With Kids, Shifts or a Home Office

by Hannah Wetherby

€2.99 · eBook (EPUB)  |  €2.99 · Digital book (PDF)

It is 7:06 and you are already behind, with your feet still off the floor? Snooze after snooze, fourteen notifications, keys nowhere to be found, a coffee gulped standing up. You do not lack willpower: you lack a morning designed for real days, the ones with short sleep, kids and a late train.

This ebook gives you three mornings instead of one : a 10-minute version for rough days, a 30-minute version for normal days and a 60-minute version for when you can. Each day you pick the size life allows, and the day counts either way. The rest is decided the evening before, in seven timed minutes.

What is inside

The evening before in 7 minutes and the three-point sheet, with the sum of how many hours a year it can give back

Alarm, light, phone, water, coffee (with the FDA reference figure), breakfast and movement in 2- and 5-minute pieces

Mornings with kids, as a couple, working from home, on shifts, and for people who are not morning people

Six complete mornings with real clock times, added up and checked

12 rituals in three sizes and a 14-morning plan with day-7 and day-14 checks

Fill-in sheets and a protocol for the bad day

Who it is for. Anyone who wants a less rushed morning without becoming a different person: workers, parents, students, people who work from home or on shifts.

An honest note. This is general information and does not replace advice from a doctor or therapist, especially about sleep, eating and mood. The people in the examples are invented and results vary from person to person.
