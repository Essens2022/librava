You love animals. Now let them pay for your early mornings, rainy walks and happiest hours.

You dream of spending your days with dogs and cats instead of at a desk, but the questions stop you cold. Do you need a license? What if a dog slips its collar, a cat goes missing or a client's key gets lost? What should you even charge? And will anyone trust a newcomer with their pet and their front door?

Pet Sitting and Dog Walking Business Made Simple is a warm, safety-first guide from a former veterinary practice receptionist who started walking neighbors' dogs on her days off and built a small local pet care business over many years. You learn whether the work truly suits you, which services to start with, how to set up legally with the right insurance, how to read animals and keep them safe on walks and visits, how to act fast in an emergency, how to handle keys and homes so clients trust you completely, and how to price fairly, find loyal local clients and grow at your own pace.
