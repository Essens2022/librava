A few polite words of Japanese turn a trip into a welcome.

You are excited about Japan, but the language looks impossible. You worry about getting lost in huge stations, ordering the wrong thing, being rude without meaning to or freezing when someone speaks to you.

Easy Japanese for Travelers is a warm, practical guide from a teacher who grew up between Osaka and Bristol and now teaches evening Japanese classes for travelers. With easy respellings and polite set phrases, you learn to greet people, handle numbers and money, ride trains, check into hotels and inns, order food, shop, visit temples and hot springs, get help and make real connections.
