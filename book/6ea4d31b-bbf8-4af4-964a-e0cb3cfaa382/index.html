When the parent who cared for you starts to need your care.

A missed bill, a fall in the kitchen, a forgotten pill. You notice changes in your mom or dad, and suddenly you are juggling doctors, paperwork, siblings, your job and your own family, with no idea where to start.

Caring for Aging Parents Made Simple gives you a calm, organized plan: spotting when help is needed, having the hard conversations, organizing medical information, making the home safer, understanding care options, getting legal and financial papers in order, sharing the load with family and protecting your own health.
