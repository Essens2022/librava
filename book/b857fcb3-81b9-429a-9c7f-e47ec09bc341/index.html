Your appetite has changed overnight. Your meals should change with it.

Four bites and you are done. The plate you used to finish now feels like a mountain, some mornings you cannot face food at all, and a few hours later you feel shaky and wonder whether you are eating enough to stay strong. Ordinary cookbooks serve portions you will never finish and diet books talk about calories you are not counting.

The Easy GLP-1 Cookbook is a warm, practical guide from a former hospital catering cook who spent years making small, tempting plates for patients with poor appetites, then relearned home cooking when her sister started an appetite-reducing medication. You learn the small plate method, protein first and sip all day, how to manage nausea, fullness and reflux with food, and you cook 70 small-portion savory recipes, from protein-first breakfasts and gentle soups to easy dinners, snacks and single freezer portions. No calorie counts, no food guilt, no weight-loss promises, just food that fits a smaller appetite.
