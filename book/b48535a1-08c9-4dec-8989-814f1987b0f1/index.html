Dating After 50

Meet People You Can Trust: Write Your Profile, Spot Scams in 3 Messages and Plan 12 First Dates

by Eleanor Whitcombe

€2.99 · Digital book (PDF)  |  €2.99 · eBook (EPUB)

Nobody warned you how much changed since you last dated. Photos are judged in seconds, strangers write to you at midnight, and some of the people who say they love you are scripts run by criminal groups. This ebook is the manual for starting again, written for adults over 50 who are single after a divorce, a loss, or a life that simply went another way.

You are not too late, and you do not have to do this alone. It is practical from the first page: the words for a warm profile, the questions for a first call, a plan for every meeting, and the sentences to use when you want to say no or go home early.

What you will find inside

22 chapters with checklists, fill-in forms and word-for-word scripts

Three sample profiles and a photo plan

The five stages of a romance scam, a three-message test and a verification routine

Figures from the FBI, FTC, Pew Research Center and national fraud-reporting centers, plus where to report a scam

12 first dates with times and example costs, from $0 to $44 for two

A safety plan with a code word and polite ways to leave

How to talk to adult children, handle money and plan your first 30 days

Who it is for. Anyone over 50 who is curious about dating again, nervous about scams, or tired of advice that stops at "just be yourself".

An honest note. This is general information, not legal, financial or medical advice, and rules differ by country. Contacts and statistics were checked in November 2026.
