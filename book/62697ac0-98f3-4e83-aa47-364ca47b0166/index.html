Burnt on the outside and raw in the middle ends today.

You fire up the grill, the burgers flare up, the chicken looks done but is pink inside, and the steak you paid good money for turns gray and tough. Grilling looks simple, until you are standing over the flames guessing.

Backyard Grilling Made Simple shows you the few techniques that make the difference: two-zone heat, a hot clean grate, the lid, and a thermometer instead of guesswork. Then it gives you more than 75 easy recipes for gas and charcoal grills, with clear heat levels, times and safe target temperatures, everyday supermarket ingredients and measurements for both US and UK kitchens.
