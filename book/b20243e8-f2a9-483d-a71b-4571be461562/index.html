You love a bargain. This book shows you how to turn that habit into a simple, honest reselling routine, one good find at a time.

Maybe you have stood in a charity shop holding something lovely and cheap, wondering if anyone online would buy it. Maybe your home is full of things you no longer need, or you want flexible extra money around family, retirement, study or a job search. But the questions stop you. What actually sells? How do you know what to pay? What if it is fake, recalled or breaks in the post? And how do you avoid a spare room full of stock nobody wants?

Reselling Secondhand Items Made Simple is a friendly, practical guide from a former bus driver who started by clearing out his late father's garage, found that old tools, records and kitchenware sold well online, and has spent years buying at car boot sales, garage sales and charity shops and reselling from a spare room. He is honest that some weeks nothing sells and that fees and postage eat into every sale, and he is strict about never selling fakes or unsafe items. Step by step, he shows you how to research sold prices, source well, prepare and photograph items, write listings that get found, price with fees in mind, pack safely, handle buyers and keep records that tell you the truth.
