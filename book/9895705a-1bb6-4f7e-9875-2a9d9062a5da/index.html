The best camera is the one in your pocket. This book shows you how to use it.

You see a beautiful moment, raise your phone, and the photo comes out dark, blurry or flat. The kids are a smudge, the sunset looks gray and the birthday cake is a yellow blob. It is not your phone. It is a handful of simple things nobody ever explained.

Stunning Photos With Your Phone is the friendly, no-jargon guide for anyone who wants better pictures of family, grandkids, pets, trips, food and gardens, without buying a camera. You will learn how light works, where to stand, which lens to pick and how to fix a photo in two minutes with the editor already on your phone.
