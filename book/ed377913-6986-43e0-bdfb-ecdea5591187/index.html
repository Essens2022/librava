Fresh vegetables from your own backyard, without the weeds, the digging or the guesswork.

Maybe you tried a few pots that dried out, or a patch of lawn that turned into a jungle of weeds by July. Raised beds change that. The soil warms faster, drains better and stays loose, the weeds are easier to keep out, and everything is within reach.

The Easy Raised Bed Vegetable Garden walks you through your first season step by step: where to put your beds, how to build and fill them, what to plant and when, and how to keep everything growing until the last harvest in autumn. Clear bed plans show exactly how many plants go in each square.
