Build Simple Habits That Stick. A Practical Step-by-Step Guide to Creating a Morning Routine You Enjoy, Building Good Habits, Breaking Bad Ones and Getting More of What Matters Done Without Burning Out

You do not need to wake up at 5am to have a great morning. You need a routine that fits your real life.

The alarm goes off, you hit snooze, then it is a rush of coffee, lost keys and a day that feels behind before it has started. You have tried big new routines before. They lasted about a week.

Better Mornings, Better Days shows you a gentler way that actually works. Written by a former shift worker and busy dad who rebuilt his own days one small habit at a time, it explains in plain words how habits form, then helps you design a morning routine that suits your schedule, start habits so small they are easy to keep, drop the ones you do not want and get back on track without guilt when life gets in the way.
