You love making a dirty car look brand new. Now imagine doing it on your own schedule, for clients who keep coming back.

Maybe you are tired of warehouse shifts, a retail rota or a boss who decides your hours. You already spend Sunday mornings on your own paintwork, but turning that into a business feels risky. Which equipment do you really need? What about insurance, water rules and chemicals? And how do you price a filthy seven-seater without working half the day for nothing?

Start a Mobile Car Detailing Business Made Simple is a practical, upbeat guide from a detailer who spent eight years valeting cars at a busy dealership, then bought a secondhand van and has run his own mobile round for over a decade. He is honest about the sore knees, the rainy weeks, the jobs he underquoted and the paint he once polished through. Step by step you build a simple service menu, choose equipment that earns its space, set up your business and insurance, kit out a safe van, keep wash water out of storm drains and work safely with chemicals. Then you master the exterior and interior detail, learn paint correction without the hype, price every job by method and build a loyal round of maintenance clients.
