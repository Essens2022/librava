You wake up and cannot turn your head. Your shoulders live somewhere near your ears. This book is the calm, simple plan you have been looking for.

By mid-afternoon the ache creeps from the base of your skull into the tops of your shoulders. You rub it, crack it, try another pillow and scroll through scary advice at midnight. Checking a blind spot hurts, your phone keeps pulling your chin down, and you wonder if something is badly wrong or if this is just how life feels now.

Neck and Shoulder Pain Relief Made Simple is a reassuring, quietly funny guide from a former newspaper sub-editor who spent eighteen years hunched over screens until one morning he could not turn his head at all. His physical therapist showed him that gentle movement, a little strength and better habits beat cracking his neck, and he now teaches free desk stretch sessions at his local library. You learn why most neck pain is not serious damage, which warning signs need help, what to do in the first days of a stiff neck, 18 gentle stretches and strength moves, and the desk, phone, sleep and stress habits that help you feel more in control of your neck and shoulders.
