Sourdough Bread for Beginners

A 7-day starter log, 47 recipes from the first loaf to rolls, pizza and discard bakes, 10 timetables and 16 fixes for flat, gummy loaves

by Margot Ellery

The jar on the counter is a gray paste, the video made it look so easy, and the loaf you baked could hold open a door.

Baking with a starter is not a talent. It is a rhythm of feeding, waiting, folding and baking, and nobody tells you the rhythm. This ebook does. It starts with an empty jar and a bag of flour, and ends with loaves you are proud to slice, in plain language and clear numbered actions.

What you will find inside

A 7-day starter, one page per day : the amounts in grams and cups, what you will see and smell, a tick-box list and a log for every feeding, plus a Day 8 to 14 rescue page, feeding ratios and 8 starter questions

47 recipes in 6 chapters : 5 starter methods, 16 loaves from a plain overnight country loaf to onion and thyme, 6 rolls and buns, 6 pizza doughs and flatbreads, and 14 discard recipes for the jar you would otherwise pour away

10 real timetables with clock times for work days, free Sundays, dinner rolls, pizza night and brunch

16 fixes for flat, dense, gummy, pale and burnt loaves, a crumb guide, 12 questions and 7 common mistakes

Baker's percentages explained in 3 pages, with scaling tables, a dough temperature calculator and 6 practice problems

Ready to print : starter logs, 12 bake log cards, a weekly planner, checklists, a master recipe card and a pantry list

Reference : conversion tables, a 41-term glossary, quick finders and an index, in 136 pages

A taste of what you will bake

A blistered overnight loaf, mixed after lunch and baked for breakfast

Pillowy dinner rolls and chewy bagels from the same jar

Crisp crackers and fluffy pancakes made from starter you were about to throw away
