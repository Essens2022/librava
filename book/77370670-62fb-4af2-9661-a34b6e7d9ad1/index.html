Tea and toast is not a meal. You deserve food that keeps you strong, tastes good and is easy to make for one or two.

Maybe your appetite has shrunk and cooking for one feels like too much bother. Maybe the stairs feel steeper, tough meat has become hard work after dental treatment, or you look in the fridge and find half a pint of milk and a lemon. You know good food matters, but every book seems to be written for a family of six or a special diet you do not have.

Healthy Eating After 60 Cookbook is written by a retired school cook who now volunteers at a weekly lunch club, and who at 67 cooks for herself and her husband Frank. She watched friends quietly eat less and less, and set out to make simple, tasty food that keeps people strong. She explains in plain words what changes after 60, how to build a strong plate with protein at every meal, calcium and vitamin D for your bones and fiber and fluids for your digestion, how to keep your kitchen safe and your shopping affordable, then gives you 66 savory recipes, from savory oatmeal and fish pie to slow-cooked beef in gravy and freezer curries, each one easy to halve for one.
