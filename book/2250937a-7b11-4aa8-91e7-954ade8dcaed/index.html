Tired of PCOS diets that leave you hungry, guilty and confused? You do not need another list of banned foods. You need a good dinner tonight.

One website says cut every carb. Another says no dairy, no gluten, no fruit. A third is selling a tea that promises to "fix your hormones". You have tried eating less and ended up shaky by noon and raiding the snack drawer by three, every meal feels like a test you are failing, and meanwhile your partner just wants to know what is for dinner.

PCOS Cookbook Made Simple is written by a former bakery shift manager who was diagnosed with PCOS in her late twenties after years of irregular periods, acne and three o'clock energy crashes. She spent years bouncing between strict internet diets that left her hungry and miserable, then settled into a calm, regular way of eating built on beans, whole grains, vegetables and protein at every meal, cooked for a partner who does not have PCOS and a firm refusal to make two dinners. She explains PCOS and insulin resistance in plain words, shows you how to build a balanced plate without weighing a thing, then gives you 70 filling savory recipes, from egg muffins and burrito bowls to curries, chili, tray bakes and packed lunches, so you can eat normal family food with no banned foods and no guilt, and feel more in control alongside the care your doctor provides.
