You do not need an hour of silence to feel calmer. You need the right few minutes.

Your mind is racing before the alarm goes off, your shoulders live up near your ears and every small thing feels like one thing too many. You have heard that mindfulness helps, but who has time to sit still for half an hour?

Calm in Minutes gives you simple, practical tools that fit into a normal busy day. Written in plain words by a former nurse who now teaches mindfulness to working people and parents, it shows you how to settle your body with your breath, steady a racing mind, handle stress at work and at home, sleep better and be a little kinder to yourself along the way.
