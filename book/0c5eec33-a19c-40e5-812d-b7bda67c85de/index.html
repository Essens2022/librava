How to Get Your CDL or HGV Licence, Choose Good Training, Pass the Medical and Your Tests, Understand Hours of Service and Drivers' Hours Rules, Drive Safely, Stay Healthy and Sleep Well on the Road and Land Your First Trucking Job, With a 7-Step Licence Timeline, a 90-Day First Job Plan and Ready-to-Use Checklists

Your next career could be behind the wheel of a truck. You just need someone who has driven the road to show you the way in.

Maybe the factory closed, the warehouse shifts wore you down or you are leaving the military and want work with structure and open sky. But every school seems to promise a guaranteed job, the licence classes read like alphabet soup and you have no idea whether the medical, the tests or the hours rules will trip you up. The last thing you want is to spend your savings on the wrong course and find out the hard way.

Become a Truck Driver Made Simple is a steady, straight talking guide from a driver who switched careers at thirty-eight when his factory closed, spent eighteen years on regional and long-haul routes and later rode along with new hires as a driver mentor. Step by step you choose the right kind of trucking work and licence class, prepare honestly for the medical, pick training that truly prepares you, get ready for your theory and practical tests and learn the hours and rest rules for your country. Then you learn to drive safely, handle loads and paperwork, live well in the cab, protect your sleep, back and family life, land your first job and get through year one with your licence clean.
