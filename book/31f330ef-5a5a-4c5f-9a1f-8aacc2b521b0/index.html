Buying your first home can feel like learning a new language. This book translates every step.

Pre-approval, deposits, closing costs, surveys, escrow, conveyancing. The first time you buy a home, the words alone can be overwhelming, and the stakes feel huge. Most first-time buyers wish someone had simply explained the whole process from start to finish.

Buying Your First Home Made Simple does exactly that. Written by a former mortgage office administrator who bought her own first home after years of renting, it walks US and UK buyers through every stage: deciding if you are ready, budgeting, saving a deposit, improving your credit, choosing a mortgage, building your team, finding the right home, making an offer, inspections and surveys, and closing day.
