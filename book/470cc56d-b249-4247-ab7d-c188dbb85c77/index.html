Told to cut the salt? You do not have to settle for bland food. You just need a cook who knows the tricks.

You came home from the checkup with a high blood pressure reading and a leaflet that says less salt. Now every meal tastes like cardboard, the family pushes their plates around, and you discover that bread, soup, sauces and nearly everything in a packet are loaded with sodium. You want to help your heart, but you also want dinner to taste like dinner.

The Easy Low Sodium Cookbook is written by a former restaurant line cook who salted everything by the pinchful for twenty years, until her husband's diagnosis sent her back to the stove to learn how to make food taste big without the shaker. She is honest that the first two weeks taste flat, then shows you how lemons, garlic, toasted spices, fresh herbs and proper browning bring the flavor back. Every one of the 70 savory recipes uses no added salt or at most 1/4 teaspoon for the whole dish, so you can eat well with your doctor's advice and feel more in control of your plate.
