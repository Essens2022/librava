It is 2:10 p.m., your jaw is clenched, and you have read the same sentence four times.

Nothing dramatic has happened. A message landed, a meeting ran over, the list grew, and your body decided the alarm should stay on. The usual advice asks for a free weekend or an hour of quiet you do not have. This ebook gives you tools for the minutes you do have: short, practical resets you can use at a desk, in a corridor or in a parked car.

The honest promise. You will not find a cure here, and no ebook can offer one. You will find simple, non-clinical tools to come down from tense moments faster, handle work pressure and overwhelm more steadily, and say a calm no without a week of guilt. It is educational, not medical advice, and it tells you plainly when to talk to a professional.

What you get inside

A 14-day plan with a morning, midday and evening step each day, plus two weekly reviews

12 reset cards in large type for the worst moments, each finished in about a minute

15 boundary scripts for work and family, with the sentence to repeat if they push

20 printable sheets , including trigger trackers, a meeting prep sheet, an overwhelm sort and a weekly reset

4 cheat sheets, 15 answers to common questions, a glossary and an index
