How to Plan a Stunning, Stress-Free Wedding You Can Actually Afford, With Step-by-Step Checklists, Budget Templates and Clever Money-Saving Ideas

A beautiful wedding is about the people, the promises and the joy, not the size of the bill.

You are engaged, you are excited, and then you see the prices. Venues, catering, flowers, photos and a dress can add up faster than you ever imagined, and everyone has an opinion about how it should be done.

The Beautiful Budget Wedding Planner shows you how to plan a stunning, meaningful day you can actually afford. Step by step, you will set your priorities, build a realistic budget, shape your guest list, find an affordable venue and save on food, attire, flowers, photos and more, with a calm 12-month timeline and checklists that keep everything on track.
