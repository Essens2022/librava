Chess is easier to learn than you think. You just need someone to explain it in the right order.

Chess Made Easy for Beginners takes you from setting up the board to playing complete, sensible games. Every rule is explained clearly, every idea comes with a simple position you can set up on your own board, and there are no long opening lines to memorize.
