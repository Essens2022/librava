Digital Detox: How to Use Your Phone Less

A 30-Day Plan to Cut Screen Time, Notifications and Doomscrolling Without Cutting Yourself Off, With 7 Rules, 5 Weekend Experiments, 40 Screen-Free Ideas and 25 Worksheets

by Naomi Hargreaves

€2.99 · eBook (EPUB)  |  €2.99 · Digital book (PDF)

Do you unlock your phone 96 times a day and not even know why? You are not weak: you are holding a tool built to pull you back in. This ebook does not ask you to throw it away. It gives you a plan to put it back in its place.

You start by looking at your real numbers: 5 hours 12 a day is 1,898 hours a year, almost 119 waking days. Then you change one thing at a time, over four weeks, with seven rules that are easy to remember and worksheets you fill in by hand. No lectures, no compulsory fasts.

What is inside

The notification sweep in ten minutes and a home screen that does not call you

Where the phone sleeps, the screen-free evening and the morning quarter hour

A day-by-day plan with averages, mood and a one-page rulebook of your own

The ten-minute protocol for when the urge hits

Social media, work, family, kids, news and sleep, with ready-to-say scripts

Five weekend experiments and forty screen-free ideas

What to do after a relapse and when to ask a professional for help

Who it is for. Anyone who feels the phone is costing them hours, sleep and attention and wants to cut it down without giving up what they need.

An honest note. This is general information about habits and screen use: it is not therapy and does not replace a doctor or psychologist. The studies mentioned have limits and we say so. The people in the examples are invented, and results vary from person to person.
