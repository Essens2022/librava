Learn to Draw What You See With Simple Shapes, Lines and Shading, Step by Step Exercises and a 30-Day Sketching Plan, Even If You Think You Cannot Draw

Drawing is not a talent. It is a skill, and you can learn it.

Maybe you stopped drawing in school because someone said you were not good at it. Maybe you still draw stick figures and wish you could sketch your dog, your garden or a cup of coffee on a quiet morning.

Drawing for Beginners Made Simple teaches you to see like an artist and draw what you see, with just a pencil and paper. Clear step by step exercises cover lines, shapes, proportion, shading and perspective, then still life, nature, animals, faces and figures, with a 30-day sketching plan.
