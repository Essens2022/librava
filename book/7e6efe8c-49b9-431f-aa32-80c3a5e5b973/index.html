The Happy Cat Handbook

A Complete Guide to Caring for Your Cat. Everything New and Experienced Owners Need to Know About Choosing, Feeding, Health, Behavior, Litter Training and Keeping Your Cat Happy at Every Age

by Hannah Brooks

€6.97 · Digital book (PDF)

Your cat is telling you what it needs every day. This book helps you understand it.

Why does your cat knock things off the table, bite during a cuddle or race around at 3am? What should you feed, which plants are dangerous and when is it time to call the vet? Whether you are bringing home your first kitten or sharing your sofa with a wise old cat, there is a lot to know.

The Happy Cat Handbook brings it all together in one friendly, practical guide. Written by a longtime cat owner and rescue fosterer who has cared for dozens of kittens and senior cats, it covers choosing the right cat, preparing your home, feeding, litter boxes, body language, play, grooming, health, common problems and every stage of life, always with your vet as your partner.
