Better coffee at home than at the café, for a fraction of the cost.

You spend a small fortune on café coffee, yet the cup you make at home tastes bitter, weak or flat. You have a brewer in the cupboard, but grind sizes, ratios and bean labels leave you guessing every morning.

Coffee at Home Made Simple is a relaxed, friendly guide from a former café barista and trainer who now teaches home brewing workshops.
