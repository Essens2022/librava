Go from never played to strumming real songs, step by step.

You have always wanted to play an instrument, but music lessons felt intimidating, guitar seemed too hard and you were not sure where to begin. The ukulele is friendly, affordable and fun, and you only need the right first steps.

Ukulele for Beginners Made Simple is a patient, upbeat course from a music teacher who runs ukulele groups for kids and retirees. You choose and tune your ukulele, learn your first chords, strum with a steady beat, change chords smoothly and play complete traditional songs, then add new keys, strumming feel, simple melodies and barre chords.
