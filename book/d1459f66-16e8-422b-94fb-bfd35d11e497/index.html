It is not too late. Adults learn to swim every single day.

You smile at the pool party but stay on the edge. You skip beach days, hold your breath when water splashes your face and feel embarrassed that you never learned. Your heart races just thinking about the deep end.

Learn to Swim as an Adult Made Simple is a patient, encouraging guide from a former lifeguard and adult swim teacher who has taught hundreds of nervous grown-ups, including his own mother at 61. Used alongside lessons, it takes you from your first steps in the shallow end to floating, gliding, breathing and swimming freestyle, backstroke and breaststroke, all with safety first.
