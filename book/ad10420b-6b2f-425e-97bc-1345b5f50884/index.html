Graphic Design for Beginners Made Simple

Learn Layout, Color and Fonts the Easy Way and Create Eye-Catching Social Posts, Flyers, Posters, Logos, Invitations, Menus and Business Cards With Free Online Tools, With 15 Hands-On Tasks and a 30-Day Design Challenge, Even If You Have Never Designed Anything Before

by Lydia Marchetti

€6.97 · Digital book (PDF)

You do not need talent or expensive software. You need a few simple principles.

Someone asked you to make a flyer, a menu or a post, and you froze. You tried a template, added five fonts and a rainbow of colors, and somehow it still looked messy. The date got lost, the photo went blurry in print and a quiet voice said you are just not creative.

Graphic Design for Beginners Made Simple is a friendly, jargon-free course from a self-taught designer who started with school bake sale flyers, worked in a small print shop and now teaches evening classes for non-designers. You learn the handful of principles behind every good design, then put them straight to work on real projects: social posts, flyers, posters, logos and brand kits, invitations and menus, and business cards, all with a free online design tool and the device you already own.
