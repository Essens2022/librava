Your knees are not worn out. They are waiting for strong muscles and a steady plan.

Stairs make you wince. Getting out of a low chair takes a heave and a groan, kneeling in the garden is out of the question and every long walk ends with an ache. Someone said "wear and tear" or "bone on bone," and now you worry that every step uses up what is left of your knees.

Knee Pain Relief Made Simple is a warm, plain speaking guide from a former school dinner lady (lunchroom supervisor) and keen hill walker who was told at 58 that she had wear and tear in both knees and nearly gave up walking. A physical therapist showed her that strong muscles and steady movement helped far more than rest, and she now runs a friendly walking and exercise group for people with sore knees. You learn how your knees really work, which warning signs need help, how much discomfort is okay, 20 simple exercises and stretches you can do in a chair, on the bed or at the kitchen counter, and the pacing, footwear and flare habits that help you feel more in control.
