Money Skills for Young Adults Made Simple

Everything Nobody Taught You About Your First Paycheck, Bank Accounts, Budgeting, Saving an Emergency Fund, Credit Scores, Student Loans, Renting Your First Place, Investing Basics and Avoiding Money Scams, With a 30-Day Money Starter Plan for Your Late Teens and Twenties

Nobody hands you a manual for money when you leave home. This is that manual.

Your paycheck lands and it is smaller than you expected. A fee appears that is bigger than the thing you bought. Friends post trips and new cars while you wonder how they do it, a landlord wants paperwork you have never heard of and a stranger online swears he can double your money. Everyone else seems to know the rules, and you are quietly afraid of getting it wrong.

Money Skills for Young Adults Made Simple is a friendly, no-lecture guide from a former bank teller (cashier) who overdrew an account in the first month of living alone and signed a regrettable phone contract and an even worse lease, then spent years running free money workshops for first-year students and apprentices. One milestone at a time, you learn to read your payslip, set up banking without fees, build a simple budget, save a starter emergency fund, use credit without falling into debt, understand student loans, rent safely, start investing slowly and spot scams, so you feel calm, capable and in control of your money in your late teens and twenties.
