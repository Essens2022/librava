Learn Pyrography Step by Step and Create Beautiful Signs, Spoons, Coasters and Keepsakes Safely at Home, With Tool Guides, Shading Techniques, Practice Sheets and Easy Projects

A warm pen, a piece of wood and a quiet evening. That is all it takes to make something lasting.

You have seen beautiful burned signs and spoons at craft fairs and wondered if you could make them. But the tools look confusing, you worry about fumes and burns and your first attempt at a straight line came out shaky.

Wood Burning for Beginners Made Simple is a patient, methodical guide from a retired joiner (carpenter) who now teaches pyrography at a community workshop and sells at craft fairs. You set up safely, choose a burner and tips, prepare the right wood, practice your first marks, transfer designs, burn crisp lines and lettering, shade with depth and make ten projects to keep or give.
