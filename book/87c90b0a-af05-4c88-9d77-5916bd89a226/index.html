You know something people need. This book shows you how to turn it into a product they can download today.

Maybe you are the teacher whose worksheets everyone borrows, the friend with the budget spreadsheet that actually works, or the organizer people keep asking for help. You have heard about selling printables and digital planners, but every video promises money while you sleep, and you worry about copyright, pricing, taxes and opening a shop that nobody ever visits.

Create and Sell Digital Products Made Simple is a friendly, organized and refreshingly honest guide from a former office administrator who made budget planners and meal planning sheets for her own kitchen table, sold her first download after three long weeks and has run a small digital products shop alongside part-time work for many years. She walks you through every step: choosing a lane from what you know, finding ideas people want, staying on the right side of copyright and licenses, designing clean printables, editable templates and simple courses, pricing with real methods, writing honest listings, delivering files smoothly and marketing without a big audience. No hype, no income promises, just a small shop built on products that genuinely help people.
