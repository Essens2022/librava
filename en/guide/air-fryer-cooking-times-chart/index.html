Air Fryer Cooking Times Chart: Temperatures and Times for 30 Foods

By the Librava editors · Updated 2026-10-26

You have food in one hand, the basket in the other, and no idea how long it needs. The short answer: most everyday foods cook at 375 to 400°F (190 to 200°C) for 8 to 25 minutes in a preheated basket air fryer, shaken or flipped halfway, with larger items like whole potatoes taking 40 minutes or more.

Below you will find a full chart for 30 common foods with temperatures in °F and °C, a reheating table, safe internal temperatures, times for frozen food, and the simple rule for turning any oven recipe into an air fryer one. The times assume a 4 to 6 quart (about 4 to 6 liter) basket model and food in a single layer.

Air fryer cooking times for meat and poultry

Meat and poultry vary most with thickness, so treat these times as a starting point and check with an instant-read thermometer in the thickest part, away from bone.

For bacon and sausages, pour 2 to 3 tablespoons of water into the drawer under the basket. It catches the fat drips and cuts down on smoke.

Air fryer times for fish and seafood

Fish cooks fast and dries out quickly, so start checking at the low end of each range. It is done at 145°F (63°C), or when it looks opaque and flakes easily with a fork.

FoodTemperatureTimeNotes
Salmon fillets, 6 oz (170 g)390°F (200°C)8 to 11 minNo need to flip
White fish fillets380°F (195°C)8 to 12 minOil well
Large raw shrimp (prawns)400°F (200°C)6 to 8 minShake halfway, pink and opaque
Frozen breaded fish fillets400°F (200°C)12 to 15 minTurn halfway
Frozen fish fingers400°F (200°C)8 to 10 minFlip halfway

Put salmon skin side down and leave it alone. The skin protects the flesh from the strong heat above and peels off easily afterward.

For crispier homemade fries, soak the cut potatoes in cold water for 30 minutes, then dry them very well with a clean towel before tossing them in a teaspoon or two of oil. Wet potatoes steam instead of crisping.

Cooking frozen food in the air fryer

Prepared frozen foods such as fries, nuggets, fish fingers, spring rolls and mozzarella sticks go straight into the basket with no thawing. A reliable rule: cook at 380 to 400°F (195 to 200°C) for about the oven time on the package minus a third, shake halfway and check a few minutes early.

  • Frozen chicken nuggets: 400°F (200°C), 8 to 10 minutes, shake halfway.
  • Frozen mozzarella sticks: 380°F (195°C), 6 to 8 minutes, do not thaw and watch closely.
  • Raw frozen chicken breasts, fish fillets or burgers: expect about 50 percent longer than fresh, and always check the internal temperature.

With raw frozen proteins, add seasoning after the first few minutes, once the surface has thawed enough for it to stick. Separate any pieces frozen together first. Large items or anything in a solid block should be thawed in the fridge, because the outside will overcook long before the center is safe.

Safe internal temperatures to check

Times on any chart are a guide. Air fryers differ in power and basket size, and a thick chicken breast takes longer than a thin one. An instant-read thermometer is the only sure way to know meat and fish are cooked.

FoodSafe internal temperatureNotes
Chicken and turkey165°F (74°C)All cuts, including ground
Ground beef and pork160°F (71°C)Burgers, meatballs, sausages
Beef, pork, lamb whole cuts145°F (63°C)Rest 3 minutes
Fish145°F (63°C)Or opaque and flaking
Leftovers165°F (74°C)Steaming hot throughout
Safety first. Keep your air fryer on a stable, heatproof surface with about 5 inches (13 cm) of space on every side, never block the rear vent and do not leave it running unattended. Only use parchment with food on top so the fan cannot lift it into the element, and never preheat with parchment inside. White smoke usually means grease or water on a hot surface; black smoke means something is burning, so switch off and unplug.

How to convert oven times to air fryer times

Most oven recipes adapt well. The starting rule is to lower the temperature by about 25°F (15°C) and cut the time by about 20 percent, then check early.

For example, a recipe that roasts at 425°F (220°C) for 30 minutes becomes about 400°F (200°C) for 24 minutes. Start checking at around 18 minutes. The air fryer cooks faster because a fan pushes hot air hard around the food from an element very close above it.

Big casseroles and deep bakes do not convert well, because the air fryer is best with smaller, shallower portions. Use a 6 or 7 inch (15 or 18 cm) cake pan or ovenproof dish that leaves space around the edges for air to move. If you have an oven-style model with racks, use the middle rack and add a minute or two.

Reheating times and tips for crispy results

The air fryer is excellent for reviving leftovers, because the moving air dries the surface while the inside warms. Spread food in one layer and preheat first.

LeftoverTemperatureTime
Pizza slices350°F (180°C)3 to 5 min
Fries and wedges375°F (190°C)3 to 5 min
Chicken pieces375°F (190°C)6 to 10 min
Roasted vegetables360°F (180°C)3 to 5 min
Fish fillets320°F (160°C)5 to 7 min

Reheat leftovers only once, and throw away anything that has sat at room temperature for more than 2 hours. Whatever you cook, these habits make the chart times work:

  • Preheat for 3 to 5 minutes, or run it empty at the set temperature for 3 minutes.
  • Pat food dry and use a light coat of high smoke point oil from a refillable mister or a brush. Skip aerosol sprays on nonstick baskets, as they build up a sticky coating.
  • Cook in a single layer with a little space; two quick batches beat one crowded one.
  • Shake small pieces every 5 minutes and flip larger pieces halfway.
  • Clean the basket and drawer after each use so old grease does not smoke next time.

Frequently asked questions

Do you need to preheat an air fryer?

For most foods, yes. Preheating for 3 to 5 minutes means the hot basket starts browning food straight away, which gives better color and less sticking. It matters most for breaded foods, meat and potatoes. If your model has no preheat button, set it to the recipe temperature and run it empty for about 3 minutes. Reheating leftovers is fine from cold.

How long do you cook chicken breast in an air fryer?

A 6 to 8 oz (170 to 225 g) boneless chicken breast usually takes 15 to 20 minutes at 375°F (190°C) in a preheated air fryer, turned halfway. Thicker breasts take longer. Always check the thickest part with a thermometer: chicken is safe at 165°F (74°C). Rest it for a few minutes before slicing so the juices settle back in.

Why is my air fryer food not crispy?

The usual cause is a crowded basket. When pieces are piled up, steam gets trapped and food softens instead of browning. Cook in a single layer, in batches if needed. Pat food dry before cooking, add a light coat of oil, preheat the basket and shake or flip halfway. For breaded foods, mist dry crumbs with oil before cooking and again when you turn them.

Can you put aluminum foil or parchment in an air fryer?

Parchment can go in only with food on top of it, so the fan cannot lift it into the heating element, and never during preheating. Perforated liners let air move better than plain sheets. If you use foil, keep it small and weighed down by food, leave space around the edges for airflow, and check your manual, since some models advise against it.

How do I convert an oven recipe to the air fryer?

Lower the oven temperature by about 25°F (15°C) and reduce the time by about 20 percent, then start checking a few minutes early. A dish roasted at 425°F (220°C) for 30 minutes becomes roughly 400°F (200°C) for 24 minutes. This works best for roasting, crisping and small bakes. Large, deep casseroles are better left in the oven.

How much food can I cook in a 5 quart air fryer at once?

In a typical 4 to 6 quart (4 to 6 liter) basket, aim for one layer with a little space between pieces. That is roughly 1 lb (450 g) of fries or about four chicken breasts at a time. Small foods such as fries can be loosely layered if you shake them every 5 minutes. Larger amounts cook better in two batches.

Pick one food from the chart that you cook often, such as frozen fries or chicken breast, cook it tonight in a preheated basket in a single layer, and write down the exact time your own machine needs. A few notes like that turn any chart into your personal, reliable guide.
